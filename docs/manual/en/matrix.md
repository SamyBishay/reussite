# The matrix

The matrix sorts your steps in four quadrants. Urgency and importance are judged **separately**.

![The matrix](shot:matrix)

## The four quadrants

- **Now** (urgent and important). A deadline within the urgency window, or something that blocks one. Today's list starts here.
- **Protect** (important, no clock). Nothing forces these. One step gets a slot every day, or they never happen.
- **Quick** (urgent, less important). Keep them small, batch them, or hand them off.
- **Later** (neither). Park them, or drop them for good.

## Urgent means a date

*Urgent* means a deadline within the **urgency window** (three weeks by default, Settings → Method), or blocking something that has one. The dates handle it on their own. Mark a step urgent by hand only when it blocks a deadline.

A far date isn't urgent however important. Neither is anything you can't act on yet: the step is the part you control, such as asking.

If you move a step into an urgent quadrant, Réussite asks: "Urgent means a date. When is it due?"

**Practice says:** most of what feels urgent is only loud. A date is what makes it urgent. [evidence: E-xx]

## Moving, filtering and searching

- Drag a step to another quadrant, or long-press it on a phone. Or open the step and use **Move to…**.
- Filter by project or suit, or search your steps.
- **In Home** shows this place's steps and the ones marked Anywhere. **Everything** shows all.
- Change a due date wherever a step appears.

## Steps

A step has a title, written as the **first action**: the app or place, then what to do there ("Open Mail and reply to…"). It also has a starter, an estimate, an optional due date, a project and a suit. Under **More** you will find the kind (a step or a decision), "Waits for", the app and link, repeats and a fixed time.

**Practice says:** a title that names the app and the action is a step you can start. "Taxes" is not. [evidence: E-xx]

## Repeating steps and standing items

A step can repeat every day, week, month or every few days, weeks or months. A **standing item** is leisure, people or care: an obligation, not a reward. With a time and weekdays (for example, choir on Wednesdays at 19:30), it appears in your plan on those days by itself, and you're told when to leave.
