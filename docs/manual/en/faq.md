# Questions people ask

## Is this therapy?

No. Réussite helps you apply a method. It doesn't replace therapy or care from a professional.

## Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts. Your deck is on your device.

## Do I need an AI?

No. By default Réussite understands you on this device: what you type on Home is read by the app itself, and nothing leaves the device. A cloud AI is optional: your own key, or your AI chat app through the connector, in Settings → How Réussite understands you. There is no copy and paste. See [AI](ai.md).

## Does it work offline?

Yes. The app and the manual's text work offline. A screenshot appears offline once you've seen it online.

## It says "This deck was saved by a newer version."

One of your devices has a newer version. This device shows your deck, but doesn't edit, until you tap **Reload**. Nothing is lost.

## Nothing happened when I typed on Home.

Réussite asks when a line is unclear. It may be asking which card you mean. If no AI is set up, a line the app can't read opens the card form already filled in. Try a verb: "call the bank tomorrow", "buy bread friday".

## I can't start a card.

Tap **I'm stuck**. Pick the friction, do just the first move, and if it still won't start, write a smaller one. See [The daily loop](daily-loop.md).

## A card keeps carrying over.

A card carried three times or more is too big, or hides a decision. When you deal the day, on its card, tap **Shrink it** or **Make it a decision**.

## I forgot to start the timer.

Tap **I started earlier** and say when you began. Or play the card and pick how long it took.

## Where did the "start" times go in my calendar?

A calendar is for what is at a time and what is on a day. Your day’s cards stay in Today. The calendar shows only fixed-time items.

## Why is only one card shown when I added five?

Cards that wait for another are one card: the first, with "+4 after". Tap it to see the others. When the first is played, the next takes its place. If you'd rather have them separate, choose **In any order** when you break a card down, or empty "Waits for" on a card.

## How do I note something I'm waiting for, like a call back?

Don't write it as a task. When you break a card down, tap **Something I'm waiting for**, or set a card's **Kind** to it. It sits in the chain before the card it holds back, with no estimate or time, and you can give it a day to be asked. When it happens, tap **It happened**. See [Projects](projects.md).

## Where did my standing items go from the matrix?

They are in the folded **Obligations** line under the grid. A time places them, not a quadrant.

## Where are Calendar, Projects and the Matrix?

A new Réussite starts with two tabs, Home and Today, and the others arrive when they have something to show: Calendar with your first card at a fixed time, Projects with your first project or your eighth card, the Matrix at twelve open cards or after your first weekly review. A line behind the bell says when one arrives. To see them all now, open Settings → Tabs and tap **Show every tab**. If you used Réussite before this change, you already have them all. See [Getting started](getting-started.md).

## Where are Places and Addresses & people?

On a new Réussite they are off. Switch on **Use Places and trips** and **Use Addresses & people** in Settings → Method. Anything you already saved is never deleted by switching them off.

## Why is there no weekly review?

On a new Réussite it waits until your deck is a week old and holds ten cards. **Start the review** in Settings → Method opens it on any day.

See [Sync and recovering a lost device](sync-and-recovery.md).

## I lost my recovery key.

If another device still has your deck, open Settings → Sync & devices there and tap **Show recovery key**. If every device and the key are gone, the deck can't be recovered. If you saved a backup file, you can restore it.

## Notifications don't arrive on my iPhone.

Add Réussite to the Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen), open it from there, and switch notifications on in Settings. Also check that you haven't blocked them in the phone's settings.

## Can someone else read my deck?

Not without your recovery key. The relay sees only scrambled data, unless you switch the connector on. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

## How do I bring my deck from Réussite 1?

Settings → Data → **Import from Réussite 1**. You see what will be imported before anything is written.

## Something broke.

Settings → About → **Copy diagnostics**, and send the report to a friend who can help. It never includes your cards, notes, names, places or keys.
