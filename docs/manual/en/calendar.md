# The calendar

The **Calendar** tab has three views: **Week**, **Month** and **Log**. Week and month are remembered.

![The week](shot:calendar)

## Week and month

Tinted days are inside the urgency window. Each day lists its launch points, its fixed-time items, the steps planned for it and the steps due on it.

- **Plan a step on a day:** drop it on the day's list, or use "Plan it on…" in its menu.
- **Give it a due date:** drop it on the day's date, or use "Due on…".
- You can also change a due date wherever a step appears.

## Add to calendar

**Today to calendar** and **This week to calendar** make a file of your launch points and fixed-time items, each with a reminder at its start. Open it with Apple Calendar or a desktop calendar app. It is a copy: later changes in Réussite do not follow.

## The calendar feed

Settings → Calendar can give your calendar app an address to subscribe to (Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and others). Your launch points and fixed-time items then stay up to date there, from a week back to six weeks ahead.

- By default it carries no step titles, only "▶ Start" and "Fixed time". If you tick **Include step titles**, titles are stored in plain text on the relay and whoever runs it can read them.
- Anyone who has the address can read the feed. Keep it to yourself. **Make a new address** and the old one stops working.
- Your calendar app decides how often it refreshes (Google Calendar can take hours). Don't rely on it for reminders. Reminders come from notifications. See [Settings](settings.md).

## The Log

The **Log** is your own record: cards played, time timed in the last seven days, your ratio, timed sessions, and the decisions you made. See [Timing and estimates](timing-and-estimates.md).
