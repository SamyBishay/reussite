# Projects

A project groups cards toward a goal. Projects are listed under the four suits, two per suit. Each shows as a small card: its name, how many cards are open, its next card on one line, and its bar if it has one. Tap a card to open the whole project there, with its goal, its cards and the form to add one (**Fold** closes it again). When a suit has more than two projects, **View all 4** opens that suit's whole list of projects, in a sheet. The Inbox, the decisions and the finished projects stay below.

On a new Réussite, Projects is not a tab at first: it arrives with your first project (not the Inbox) or at eight cards (see [Getting started](getting-started.md)).

## The goal

A goal has four parts. Open the project to set them:

- **The goal, in one sentence**: the outcome you want ("Draft of the thesis handed in").
- **How you'll see progress**: **A count**, **Milestones** or **No bar**.
  - *A count* is a number towards a target, with a unit: 10 pages, 12 sessions.
  - *Milestones* are a few stages you tick off yourself: "Draft sent", "Feedback in", "Final filed".
  - *No bar* means the card shows the goal and no bar. Every project starts here.
- **Starts** and **Due**: the two dates.
- **The five SMART questions** (specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, dated) are still there, folded under the goal. Fill them in if they help you; nothing depends on them.

If an AI proposed a goal, it stays "proposed" until you confirm or rewrite it.

### What moves the bar

The bar is filled by cards, not by the number of cards played. Open a card of a project that has a bar and answer **Moves the goal?** with **Yes** or **No**. Only the cards you say **Yes** to count, and only once they are played:

- With a count, each such card adds 1 when you play it. A small sheet then asks if it added more ("+5 pages"), or you keep +1. A card can also hold its own amount in the card sheet (**Adds**).
- With milestones, playing such a card asks **Tick a milestone?** and lists the ones still open. Pick one, or **Not now**: the bar only moves when you tick a milestone, here or in the project.

The weekly review asks about every open card of a project once a week, so the answers do not depend on you remembering. A card you never answered counts as No, and the bar never counts cards just because they are played. If you un-play a card, a count goes back down. Cards you add in the project, on Today or by talking to an AI can all say whether they move the goal.

**Research says:** Setting a clear goal and deciding when and where to act helps people follow through; the SMART checklist itself has not been tested. [See the evidence](research-9.md).

**Practice says:** a goal you can't tell is finished isn't a goal, and a bar that fills for any card played isn't telling you about the goal.

Older projects keep their goal as the sentence and have no bar until you pick a measure. What you wrote in the five SMART lines is kept.

## The next card

Each project should have a concrete next card, written as a first action. Add one under the project: "Next card: Open … and …".

## Order and chains

Drag a project's open cards by their grip to change their order. When a card waits for another, only the first shows as a card elsewhere (Today, the matrix), with "+n after" under its title. Here, in the project, every card is listed.

Some things in a chain are not cards to do but things you wait for, like the bellman calling back. Add one with **Something I'm waiting for** when you break a card down, or choose it as a card's **Kind**. It has no estimate, no time and no card of its own: in the chain and in the project it shows as "Waiting for: …", and the cards behind it wait for it. You can give it a day to be asked about (see [The daily loop](daily-loop.md)). When it happens, tap **It happened** and pick the day (today, or one in the past): the cards behind it are freed and go into today's hand. Dropping it frees them too. Everything you are waiting for is on the **Waiting for** screen, in the Home menu.

If your places have areas, a suit's projects are grouped under the area of their place.

## Statuses

A project is **Active**, **Paused**, **Done** or **Dropped**. When the last card is played, the project is complete and goes to the shelf, **Completed**, where it stays browsable.

## The Inbox

Cards added on the fly land in the **Inbox**. Sort them into projects at the weekly review.

## Decisions

Decisions are no longer listed here: they are on the **Matrix** tab, under the four boxes. A decision linked to a project still shows inside that project's card. See [Decisions](decisions.md).

## Places

A project can belong to a place, or to Anywhere. See [Places and trips](places-and-trips.md).

To start a project, tap the round **+** button on Projects: give it a name and a suit (the place is under **More**). Finished projects are folded away under **Closed**; tap it to open the list.
