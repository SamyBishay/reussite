# The research behind the method

Réussite follows nine rules, taken from a cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) method for adults with ADHD. This section says, for each rule, what research has and has not shown. We opened every source listed here and read what it says before putting it on a page. We read abstracts and publisher records, not full texts.

Two things first.

- **Réussite does not treat ADHD.** It helps you apply a method. It doesn't replace therapy or care from a professional.
- **No study has tested this app, or the nine rules together.** What exists is research on parts of the idea, mostly in other groups of people. Each page says which.

## The grades

- **Well supported:** tested directly, in the people the app is for, with consistent results. No rule earns this.
- **Some support:** the idea, or something close to it, has been tested and helped, but not in exactly this form, or not in adults with ADHD.
- **Practice only:** therapists teach it and many people find it useful, but we found no test of it.

A grade is a statement about the evidence, not about whether a rule is worth trying. Several rules marked practice only are still sensible things to try.

## The nine rules

1. [Write everything down](research-1.md): some support.
2. [Judge urgency and importance separately](research-2.md): practice only for the four boxes; some support for the reasons behind them.
3. [Estimate every card](research-3.md): some support that people misjudge time; practice only for estimating as a remedy.
4. [Time it, and compare](research-4.md): practice only. One set of experiments found that feedback did not reduce the bias.
5. [Break things down, and give every card a first move](research-5.md): some support.
6. [Make progress visible](research-6.md): some support for seeing progress; practice only for the plain, facts-only tone.
7. [Leisure and people are obligations](research-7.md): some support.
8. [Decisions get a method of their own](research-8.md): practice only for the weighted sheet; some support for the problem-solving steps around it.
9. [Goals are SMART](research-9.md): some support for clear goals and for deciding when and where to act; the SMART checklist itself was not tested.

## The method as a whole, and the weekly review

[The method as a whole](research-method.md): trials of CBT skills programmes for adults with ADHD show real but modest gains. Whether an app alone gives the same gains is not known. The weekly review is practice only.

## How to read a page

Each page has the rule in a sentence, its grade, what the studies found, what they do not show and the sources, each with a link. Numbers are given only where the source states them. If a source could not be confirmed, the page says so.
