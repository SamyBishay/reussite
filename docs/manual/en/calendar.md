# The calendar

The **Calendar** tab is a real calendar. It has the views **Day**, **Week** (or **3 days** on a phone), **Month**, **Agenda** and **Log**. The one you pick is remembered.

![The week](shot:calendar)

![A day, with its hours](shot:calendar-day)

## What a day shows

- **Things with a time** sit on the hour grid, from the hour they start, with their length. If there is no length, the estimate is used, or an hour.
- **The row at the top** holds the rest of the day: the cards planned for it, the steps due on it, and the cards you played. Done ones are struck through.
- A **half day** you chose in the morning check-in shows as a small label: "Afternoon · Heliopolis".
- Each event shows the step's **short name** and, when there is room, the address or place. There are no launch points here.
- Steps that wait for another are one card, the first step, with "+n after". Tap it to see the rest.

Tap an event to open the step. Tap a day's number to open that day.

## Moving things

- **Plan a step on another day:** drag its card in the top row to that day.
- **Change a time:** drag a timed step on the grid. It moves in steps of 15 minutes.
- **Make a step due on a day:** open it and choose **Move to…**.
- On a phone, tap a step to open it, then use **Move to…**.

If your place has areas, the **area chips** narrow the calendar to one area, plus what applies anywhere.

## Add to calendar

**Today to calendar** and **This week to calendar** make a file with your fixed-time items (each with a reminder at its start), and your planned and due steps as all-day events. It includes short names, places and notes. Open it with Apple Calendar or a desktop calendar app. It is a copy: later changes in Réussite do not follow.

## The calendar feed

Settings → Calendar can give your calendar app an address to subscribe to (Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and others). Your fixed-time items and your planned and due steps stay up to date there, from a week back to six weeks ahead. Steps that wait for another are left out until their turn.

**What the feed says** has three levels:

- **Times only.** It says only "Fixed time" and "Planned step", and when. This is the default.
- **Short names.** Each step's short name.
- **Short names, places and notes.** Also the place or address, the title, the note and the link, and a link that opens the step in Réussite.

- On the last two levels what the feed says is stored in plain text on the relay, and whoever runs it can read it. Réussite warns you.
- Anyone who has the address can read the feed. Keep it to yourself. **Make a new address** and the old one stops working.
- Your calendar app decides how often it refreshes (Google Calendar can take hours). Don't rely on it for reminders. Reminders come from notifications. See [Settings](settings.md).

## The Log

The **Log** is your own record: cards played, time timed in the last seven days, your ratio, timed sessions, and the decisions you made. See [Timing and estimates](timing-and-estimates.md).
