# The matrix

The matrix sorts your steps in four boxes, always two by two, small enough to be on a phone screen at once. Each box shows its name and how many steps it holds on one line (press and hold the name, or hover, for what it means), its first three steps as small cards (the suit and the title on up to two lines, a red dot when its date is late or near) and **View all** for the rest; every step is also in one list, folded at the bottom. Urgency and importance are judged **separately**. On the review day the **Start the review** box is at the top, above the four boxes.

![The matrix](shot:matrix)

## The four quadrants

- **Now** (urgent and important). A deadline within about three weeks, or something that blocks one. Today's list starts here.
- **Protect** (important, no clock). Nothing forces these. One step gets a slot every day, or they never happen.
- **Quick** (urgent, less important). Keep them small, batch them, or hand them off.
- **Later** (neither). Park them, or drop them for good.

## Decisions

Under the four boxes (and above the folded lists) are your decisions, in compact rows grouped as **To check**, **Open** and **Decided**; the closed ones are folded under **Closed (n)**. **+ New decision** is beside the heading. See [Decisions](decisions.md).

## Urgent is a tick

*Urgent* means a deadline within about three weeks, or blocking something that has one, and **you tick it**. Réussite doesn't infer it from dates: a step due tomorrow that you haven't ticked stays in Protect or Later. Its date only orders it inside its box, nearest first.

A far date isn't urgent however important. Neither is anything you can't act on yet: the step is the part you control, such as asking.

Moving a step to another box just sets its two ticks.

## Moving, filtering and searching

- Drag a step to another box, or long-press it on a phone. Or tap the step to open it and use **Move to…**. The small cards have no buttons of their own.
- **View all 5** under a box opens that quadrant's steps in a sheet, each with its buttons (the **⇄** move button, the date).
- **All cards** at the bottom opens one list of every step, each with its quadrant named. Search, and filter by project or suit, there.
- The matrix shows the place you chose to view in the place pill (see [Places and trips](places-and-trips.md)): that place's steps and the ones marked Anywhere, or every step with **Everywhere**.
- Change a due date wherever a step appears.
- If you view one of the place's **areas**, the matrix shows the steps set to that area alone (not the place's own, not Anywhere's).
- A step that waits for another shows once, as one card: the first step, with "+2 after" under its title. Tap it to unfold the rest, greyed. When the first is done, the next takes its place.

## Steps

A step has a title, written as the **first action**: the app or place, then what to do there ("Open Mail and reply to…"). It also has an estimate and an optional due date, and you say whether it is urgent and important. A starter, a project and a suit, a place, a note and the rest are under **More**. A step can also have a **short name** (five or six words, like "Choir · Hill House"). That is what calendars show. Leave it empty and Réussite makes one from the title.

Opening a step shows its buttons (Save, Mark done, Move to…, Break it down, Drop and the others) in a row under the title, always in reach. Under the title are **Time** and **Until**: pick a fixed time and when it ends on your phone's own clock (the length is worked out for you, "Lasts 3 h 30"; moving the start keeps it). Under **More** you will find the short name, the starter, the suit, the place, the project, the note, the kind (a step or a decision), "Waits for", the app and link, repeats, and what a standing item needs (its weekdays and, if it ends, its last day). When something under More is set, it says so ("More · 3 set"). "Leave early" appears once there is a time.

## Repeating steps and standing items

A step can repeat every day, week, month or every few days, weeks or months. A **standing item** is leisure, people or care: an obligation, not a reward. With a time and weekdays (for example, choir on Wednesdays at 19:30), it appears in your plan on those days by itself, and you're told when to leave. Make it in the morning for the evening and tonight's one is on your list as soon as you save it, once. To end a series, set **Until** in its More section ("until 31 December"): that is its last day, and after it nothing is placed, shown on the calendar or notified. A repeating step has the same end date in its Repeats field. Leave it empty and the series goes on until you drop it.

Standing items are not in the quadrants, because a time places them and not urgency. The matrix lists them under the grid, in a folded line called **Obligations**.
