# What's new

## 2.0 — in progress
- Home is now where you start. It greets you with only your next step. When something has a fixed time, it takes over shortly before you need to leave and says "leave at…". Under it, one box: type or dictate "call the bank tomorrow", "dentist Friday 14h", "done the report", "move the photos to Monday" or "drop the games", in English or French.
- One simple change happens at once and tells you what it did, with Undo: adding a step, ticking one done, moving a date. Anything bigger (dropping a step, a decision, several changes, anything that touches your notes) opens a screen first so you can tick what you want. If a line is unclear, Réussite asks which step you mean, or how long a step took, or opens the step form already filled in.
- Undo takes back what the line did, even from another device, and leaves alone anything you changed afterwards. History (in Home's menu) lists the last 90 days of what you typed and what came of it, with Undo.
- The tabs are now a bar at the bottom: Home, Today, Matrix, Projects, Calendar. Settings, Places, Memory and History open from the menu at the top of Home.
- The Log is now a view of Calendar (Week, Month, Log), and your decisions are in Projects.
- Memory: short notes about the people, places and things in your life, one line per fact, in your words. Add them yourself, import the memory files of Claude Code or claude.ai, or export everything to give to any AI. Mark a note Private and no AI ever sees it, not the AI in the app, nor Claude through the connector. Forget really deletes: the words are gone, including from your history and your synced copy.
- The AI now knows your notes. When you ask something, the notes that match what you wrote go with it, and "Show me what's sent" lists them. You choose how much memory a request may carry in Settings → AI. An AI can suggest a new note or a correction, and you tick it first, like any other change.
- Settings → AI shows what this month's AI has cost on this device, and you can set a monthly limit. At the limit the AI stops, and Home goes back to the step form. Only calls with a known price count toward it.
- Claude's connector can now read your memory when you mention someone or something, without ever seeing your private notes.
- A one-tap link from an AI now opens on Home with the changes to tick, and what you apply can be undone from there or from History.
- Notifications: Settings → Notifications can remind you at the right minute, even when Réussite is closed: when to start, when to restart, when to leave, the morning check-in, closing the day, the weekly review, a decision to check, and "Still on…?" when a timer runs long. On an iPhone, add Réussite to the Home Screen first. Each notification is scrambled on your device; by default it says only "Start" or "Leave now", and you can choose to include titles. Reminders are renewed each time you open the app, so if you don't open it for about two weeks they run out.
- Fixed times with a lead time and weekdays: a standing item such as a weekly rehearsal can have an hour, a "leave early by" time and the days it happens. It appears in your plan on those days by itself, and you're told when to leave even if you hadn't planned the day.
- The plan now lists fixed-time items first, with the time, the place and "Leave at…", and Swap and Cut never remove them.
- Add to calendar: in the Calendar tab, send today's or the week's launch points and fixed-time items to your calendar app as a file, each with a reminder at its start.
- Calendar feed: Settings → Calendar can give your calendar app an address to subscribe to (Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and others), so today's launch points and fixed-time items show up there and stay up to date. By default it carries no step titles; including them is your choice, and the relay can then read them. Make a new address at any time and the old one stops working.
- Delete everywhere now also switches off this device's notifications and calendar feed. The relay forgets a feed that is not renewed for 60 days.
- "Still on…?" now uses the same rule in the app and in the notification: after twice your usual time for the step (at least 45 minutes), or when the next day starts where you are.
- Settings → About's privacy note now covers notifications (the relay sees each device's push address and when notifications are due, not their text) and the calendar feed.
- Sync between your devices, if you want it: Settings → Sync & devices. Réussite makes a recovery key (letters and a QR code); another device joins by scanning it or typing it. Your list is scrambled on your device before it is sent, so only your devices can read it. It catches up when you open the app, and a couple of seconds after you change something.
- Keep the recovery key somewhere safe. If you lose every device and the key, the list is gone, and nobody can get it back. A card on Today stays until you say you saved it.
- Sync & devices shows when it last synced, your devices (you can name this one), the recovery key on request, and lets you turn sync off (your list stays) or delete the synced copy.
- If one of your devices runs a newer version than another, the older one stops editing, shows your list read-only and asks you to reload; nothing is lost or overwritten.
- Backups can be encrypted when sync is on. With sync off, Réussite reminds you every two weeks to save a backup file.
- Claude (and other AI apps that accept connectors) can read and edit your list: switch the connector on in Settings, add its address in the AI app, and type the short code it shows. Adding a step, ticking one done or moving a date happens at once, with Undo; anything bigger is shown to you first and waits for your yes. Switching it off signs everything out immediately. While it is on, the relay can read your list, and the privacy note says so.
- Settings → Connector shows the address to add in your AI app, a pairing code with a countdown, when it was last used, and a Switch off that signs every AI app out. It needs sync, and before you switch it on it says plainly that whoever runs the relay can read your list while it is on.
- A one-tap link: paste one sentence (Settings → AI) into your AI's custom instructions, and it can answer with a link instead of a code block. Opening it where your list is shows the changes to tick. On an iPhone, where links open in Safari, the changes wait for you and appear the next time Réussite syncs. Nothing is applied without your ticks.
- Settings → About now has a privacy note: what the relay can see, what an AI provider sees, and that losing every device and the recovery key loses the list.
- Copy diagnostics: in Settings → About, and on error screens, Réussite shows a short report (versions, device, errors) that you can copy to a friend when something breaks. It never includes your steps, notes, names, places or keys.
- AI, if you want it: choose OpenRouter (or any OpenAI-compatible service) or Anthropic in Settings → AI and add your key. The key stays on this device.
- Change the list in plain words: type "drop the games; the photos are due Friday" on Today, the Matrix or Projects, then tick the changes you want. Nothing is written until you confirm.
- Ask any AI: copy a summary of the method and part of your list, with your question, into any chatbot. Paste its reply back and tick the changes you want.
- Plan the day with AI: after the morning check-in, "Improve with AI" can reorder the plan, pick between steps, write starters and split big steps. If its plan breaks the method's rules (one Protect step, one obligation, your step limit), Réussite says why and keeps the plan made by the rules. You tick any starters or new steps first.
- Break it down with AI: it proposes small steps with starters and estimates; keep the ones you want and change anything before saving.
- Decision help: the AI can turn a few sentences into the problem lines, suggest options you missed, suggest pros and cons (marked "suggested", weight 3 until you set it), and draft the steps and a SMART goal. It never sets weights.
- Stuck? The AI can suggest a smaller starter, which you keep or change.
- In the weekly review, the AI can propose a SMART goal and next steps for a project. A goal stays "proposed" until you confirm it.
- Before Réussite first sends something to an AI, it shows you exactly what will be sent. You can ask to see it every time.
- Plan the day by hand: a short morning check-in (yesterday, today's shape), then a plan made by the method's rules, which you can swap or cut.
- Break a step down, or tell Réussite you're stuck: it asks which friction, shows the starter, and helps you restart.
- Decisions, start to finish: the problem, every option, weighted pros and cons of doing and of not doing, scores, the choice, and the steps. Réussite reminds you to check it on the date you set.
- The weekly review, one question at a time, ending with how your estimates compared with reality.
- Estimates that learn your pace: after five timed steps, each estimate shows what it usually takes you.
- A Calendar tab, by week or by month. Plan a step on a day, or give it a due date, by dragging or from its menu.
- Places and stays: give each place its own page, and set dates for a trip. Réussite switches place on the first day, and "before I leave" steps turn urgent on their own.
- Repeating steps, steps done on another day, and every timed session can be fixed. Forgot to start or stop the timer? It takes two taps.
- Move steps between the matrix's quadrants, filter, search, and change due dates wherever a step appears.
- Projects get an editor, SMART goals, and a shelf for finished ones.
- Closing the day deals your cards out one by one, readable, with fireworks and a little sound.
- Réussite now runs on its own, without Claude: on iPhone, iPad, Android and computers, and it works offline.
- Your list lives on your device. Settings → Data saves a backup file and restores one.
- Bring your list over from Réussite 1: Settings → Data → Import from Réussite 1. You see what will be imported before anything is written.
- When you mark a step done without the timer, Réussite asks how long it took, with buttons sized to your estimate, a wheel for anything else, and "I didn't time it".
- Links on a step open the website in your browser or the app itself, not a page inside Réussite.
- Choose your theme (light, dark, system or by time of day), colours and text size in Settings.
- French and English.
