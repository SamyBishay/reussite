# Settings

Open **Settings** from Home's menu. It is a plain page with its own heading. Everything you choose here is a setting that stays: an update never resets it.

Each section is folded: only **Appearance** is open to begin with, and a tap on a heading opens another. Inside a section, the few controls most people use are in view and the rest are under **More**. If something under More has been set to a value, More says how many ("More · 2 set"), so nothing set is ever out of sight.

![Settings](shot:settings)

## Language

English or Français. It changes at once.

## Appearance

- **Theme:** System, Light, Dark, or By time of day (you set when light starts and dark starts).
- **Palette** and **Accent colour**, and the **Suit colours**. New people start with the **af Klint** palette. If you chose one before, it stays. Réussite warns you when a colour is hard to read (aim for a contrast of 4.5 to 1).
- **Text size:** Small, Medium or Large.
- **Reduce motion**.
- **Celebrations:** Off, Calm or Lively. **Sound** and **Volume**.
- **Open websites inside Réussite.** Off by default: links open in your browser or the app itself.
- **Export theme** and **Import theme** share a look with a friend.

## Method

The numbers the method runs on, with sensible defaults:

- **Urgency window (days).** Three weeks by default. A due date inside it makes a step urgent.
- **Day starts at.** 05:00 by default. A late night still counts as the same day until then.
- **Most work steps per day.** Three by default.
- **Planning time (min).** 15 by default.
- **Default start** and **Default restarts:** your usual launch points.
- **Suits:** rename the four kinds of cards and choose their symbols.
- **Places**, their **areas** and **Stays.** See [Places and trips](places-and-trips.md).
- **Use Addresses & people**, and the **address categories.** See [Addresses and people](addresses-and-people.md).
- Times for an **obligation**, the **weekly review day and time**, the **morning check-in** and **closing the day**. Notifications use them.

## Notifications

Reminders that reach your phone at the right minute, even when Réussite is closed. On an iPhone or iPad, add Réussite to the Home Screen first. Notifications don't work without it.

Switch on **Notify me on this device**, then choose what to be notified about:

- **Start and restart:** your launch points (notifications only; the calendar doesn't carry them).
- **Time to leave:** before a fixed-time item, with the minutes of notice you choose.
- **Morning check-in** and **Close the day.**
- **Weekly review.**
- **Decision check.**
- **Still on a step?**: when a timer runs long.

By default a notification says only "Start" or "Leave now". You can choose **With titles**. Either way the text is scrambled on your device, and only your device can read it: the relay and the push service see neither. **Send a test notification** checks that it works.

Reminders are renewed each time you open the app. If you don't open it for about two weeks, they run out.

## Calendar

Subscribe to your fixed-time items from a calendar app. Choose **What the feed says**: times only, short names, or short names, places and notes. See [The calendar](calendar.md).

## Data

- **Export a backup** saves a file with your whole list.
- **Restore a backup** merges a backup into what is on this device. Newer edits here win, and a backup of what was here before is kept.
- **Export an encrypted backup** (when sync is on) opens only on a device that holds your recovery key.
- **Import from Réussite 1** brings your list over from the first version. You see what will be imported before anything is written.
- **Keep my list on this device** asks the browser not to clear your list when space runs low.

If sync is off, Réussite reminds you every two weeks to save a backup file. It is your safety net.
