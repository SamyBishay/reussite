# The daily loop

The loop is short: plan the day, start the first step, close the day. Once a week there is a review.

## Plan the day

Open Home and tap **Plan the day**. It is a short morning check-in, one question per screen.

1. **Yesterday.** Tick what was done but not ticked, with roughly how long it took. The rest carries over to today. Réussite says nothing about what didn't happen.
2. **Today's shape.** What kind of day is it (a usual day, afternoon only, an hour or less, a rest day)? When can you start? What is fixed today? If one of your places has areas, **Where will you be?** lets you pick one for the morning and one for the afternoon (see [Places and trips](places-and-trips.md)).
3. **The plan.** Réussite builds it from the method's rules. Tap **Swap** to pick something of the same weight, or **Cut** to take something off. It stays on your list for another day. Tap **Go with this** when you are happy.

![The plan, made by the method's rules](shot:checkin)

What the plan contains:

- Steps that are fixed in time come first, with "Leave at…".
- Then the **Now** steps for the place you're in.
- Then exactly one **Protect** step.
- Then **Quick** steps, if there is room.
- At most three work steps (you can change the limit in Settings → Method).
- One obligation, with a time: leisure, going out or seeing people.
- If you said where you'll be in the afternoon, the steps of that area come together at the end of the list, and the morning's area first. The plan shows "Afternoon in Heliopolis" on them.
- A little time for the planning itself.

Réussite adds up the estimates and shows what the day will probably take for you.

## Launch points

A launch point is a time to begin, or to come back to the list after a break. Typical: a start at 09:30 and a restart at 14:00. They are not a timetable. Steps float between them. On Today they are folded under **Launch points** (the summary lists the times); open it to add or remove one. The defaults in Settings → Method. They live in Réussite and its notifications. They are not in the calendar, which keeps only what is at a fixed time.

## Start

Today shows the first step on a card, **Start here**. It shows the step's starter: the smallest first action. Tap **Start the timer**, or **Already done** if you did it already. Forgot to start? **I started earlier** takes two taps. **Drop it**, tapped twice, drops a step that no longer matters; it waits in the Cemetery. Start here is always there: with nothing open on today's list, it shows the step your list would pick next, with **Start the timer**, **Put it first** and **Choose from your list**.

Today's list is the day's cards, and that includes the things at a fixed time: the dentist at 15:00 is a card on the list, with its time, and the same step is on the calendar. A change in one is a change in the other. **From your list**, **Leisure & people**, **Plan the day** and **I'm stuck** are above the list.

![Today: the first step and the rest of the list](shot:today)

**Start here follows the clock.** It is the first step that fits before your next fixed time, in the order of your list. A step that does not fit the gap waits for the next one, and a shorter step further down moves up; a step is never cut in two. When the next thing at a fixed time gets close, it takes over: Start here becomes that item, with its time and when to leave. That happens when the step you are on (what is left of it if the timer is running, or its estimate if not), plus a margin of a fifth of its length and at least 5 minutes, would run into the time you need to leave. A step with no estimate counts as 15 minutes. Something at a fixed time whose time has passed leaves the order. Home, Today's card and list, and the next-step bar on the other tabs all follow this one order. Today's list shows when each step is expected to start ("≈10:40"); that is a projection that moves with the clock, not a promise. Dragging a step by its grip still sets the order of your list. Things at a fixed time keep their time, wherever you drop them.

**Done steps fold.** Under the list, the steps you ticked off are gathered in a row, "3 done ▸". Tap it, or press Enter on it, to see them; they are closed by default. Projects fold their done steps the same way.

Adding a step by hand to today (the round **+** button, **Add to today**, **From your list**) puts it right after the step you are timing, or before the first one not done yet. It doesn't wait at the end behind the whole plan.

You can drag a step by its grip to put the list in another order. The arrows still work. When a step has areas, the list is grouped under the area's name.

When you finish a step that others were waiting for, the next one takes its place right after it on the list.

If you gave something you are waiting for a day to be asked about, a **Check: …** row appears on Today from that day, and stays until you answer. **It happened** asks which day and frees the steps behind it. **Still waiting** asks again in 3 days, in a week or on a day you pick. **Drop it** gives up waiting. It is never a card on the list, and with no day it never comes by itself.

If a step comes with an app or a link, the button opens it for you and starts the timer.

## Pause and stuck

Tap **Pause** to stop. Leave yourself the next verb ("write the second paragraph") so starting again takes no thought, and say why you stopped.

Tap **I'm stuck** when it won't start. Réussite asks which friction it is:

- **Can't start.** It's there and nothing happens.
- **Stopped and can't get back in.** There was a break and now it won't restart.
- **Drifted.** Attention went elsewhere.

Then it shows the starter, or helps you write a smaller one. If the same step stalls twice, it probably hides a decision or missing information. Réussite asks you to turn it into a question to someone, or a decision.

## Break it down

A step over 30 minutes is too big. Tap **Break it down**: it asks for the first action (the app or place, then what to do there), its starter, a time, and what comes next. It then asks whether the steps go **In this order** or **In any order**. In this order, each step waits for the one before, and they stay together in one card: you see the first, with "+3 after" under it that unfolds the rest. In any order, they are separate steps. See [Timing and estimates](timing-and-estimates.md) for the starter test.

## Close the day

Tap **Close the day**. You see the cards you played and the time you logged. Pick tomorrow's first step, and, if you like, when to start it. Your cards are dealt out one by one. Under Settings → Appearance you can switch the celebration off, calm or lively.

Réussite states facts ("4 cards, 2 h 10"). It never praises your character, and it has no streaks.

## The weekly review

Once a week (Sunday by default), Home offers **Start the review**. It takes about 45 minutes, one question at a time:

1. What changed between the quadrants?
2. What no longer matters?
3. Sort the inbox into projects.
4. Give each project a concrete next step and a SMART goal.
5. Pick the week's Protect steps and their days.
6. Name the week's obligations: people, going out, leisure.
7. Queue the decisions to make, with dates.
8. Check decisions whose date has come.

It ends with how your estimates compared with reality.
