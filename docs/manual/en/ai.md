# AI

An AI is **optional**. Réussite works fully without one. If you want one, you choose it, and you can use any provider.

![Ask any AI](shot:askany)

## Three ways to use an AI

1. **Your own key, inside the app.** In Settings → AI, choose OpenRouter (or any OpenAI-compatible service), or Anthropic, and paste your key. The key stays on this device. It is never synced or put in backups. Tap **Test the connection**.
2. **Ask any AI, with no key.** Copy a summary of the method and the part of your list you choose, with your question, into any chatbot you already use. This needs no setup and no account with Réussite.
3. **The connector.** An AI app you already use, such as Claude, reads and edits your list while you chat. See below.

## What an AI can do

With a key, Réussite can:

- turn plain words into changes ("drop the games; the photos are due Friday"): the **Change the list** box on Today, the Matrix and Projects, and the one box on Home;
- **Improve with AI**: reorder the day's plan, pick between steps, write starters and split big steps. If its plan breaks the method's rules, Réussite says why and keeps the plan made by the rules;
- **Break it down with AI**: propose small steps with starters and estimates;
- help with decisions: draft the problem, suggest options you missed, suggest pros and cons (marked "suggested", weight 3 until you set it), draft the steps and a SMART goal. It never sets weights;
- suggest a smaller starter when you're stuck;
- propose a SMART goal and next steps in the weekly review. A goal stays "proposed" until you confirm it.

**You see every change before it is written.** Tick what you want, untick what you don't. Nothing is applied without your ticks, and what you apply can be undone.

## Ask any AI and Paste the reply

1. Tap **Ask any AI** (on Today, the Matrix or Projects). Choose what to include (Today or the matrix) and write your question.
2. **Copy**, or **Share**, and paste it into any chatbot. **See the text** shows exactly what you're copying.
3. When it answers, copy its whole reply and tap **Paste the reply**. Réussite finds the block labelled reussite and shows each change before writing anything.

The reply can be one tap: in Settings → AI, **One-tap link** gives you one sentence to paste into your AI's custom instructions. The AI then answers with a link. Opening it where your list is shows the changes to tick.

## Show me what's sent

Before Réussite first sends something to an AI, it shows you **exactly what will be sent**: the endpoint, the model, the instructions, your data and request, and the memory notes. Nothing leaves the device until you tap **Send**. Turn on **Always show what's sent** to see it every time.

## How an AI should work with you

Settings → AI has a box, **How an AI should work with you**. It is a few lines of your own: one question at a time, facts about progress and no praise of character, no guilt, push back on avoidance, how long answers should be. It goes with every request and into the text Ask any AI copies. Empty it to send nothing.

## What it costs

Settings → AI shows this month's calls and tokens on this device, and about how much was spent when a price is known. Set a **monthly limit**: at the limit the AI stops, and Home goes back to the step form.

## The connector

The connector lets an AI app (such as Claude) read your list and edit it while you chat. It needs sync. In Settings → Connector:

1. Tap **Switch on**. Read what it says: **while the connector is on, whoever runs the relay can read your whole list.**
2. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Paste the address shown, and sign in.
3. Type the **pairing code** the app shows. It works once, for ten minutes.

One small change (adding a step, ticking one done, moving a date) goes straight in, with an undo. Anything else waits for your yes in the chat. **Switch off** signs every AI app out at once. Notes you marked Private are never readable by it.
