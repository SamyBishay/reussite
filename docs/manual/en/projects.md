# Projects

A project groups steps toward a goal. Projects are listed under the four suits. A project card shows its goal, how many steps are done, and its next step.

![Projects, with decisions below](shot:projects)

## The goal

Give every active project a **SMART** goal: specific, measurable, attainable, relevant and dated. Open the project and fill in the five lines. The card shows "SMART 3/5" until all are there. If an AI proposed a goal, it stays "proposed" until you confirm or rewrite it.

## The next step

Each project should have a concrete next step, written as a first action. Add one under the project: "Next step: Open … and …".

## Order and chains

Drag a project's open steps by their grip to change their order. When a step waits for another, only the first shows as a card elsewhere (Today, the matrix), with "+n after" under its title. Here, in the project, every step is listed.

Some things in a chain are not steps to do but things you wait for, like the bellman calling back. Add one with **Something I'm waiting for** when you break a step down, or choose it as a step's **Kind**. It has no estimate, no time and no card of its own: in the chain and in the project it shows as "Waiting for: …", and the steps behind it wait for it. You can give it a day to be asked about (see [The daily loop](daily-loop.md)). When it happens, tap **It happened** and pick the day (today, or one in the past): the steps behind it are freed and go on today's list. Dropping it frees them too. Everything you are waiting for is on the **Waiting for** screen, in the Home menu.

If your places have areas, a suit's projects are grouped under the area of their place.

## Statuses

A project is **Active**, **Paused**, **Done** or **Dropped**. When the last step is done, the project is complete and goes to the shelf, **Completed**, where it stays browsable.

## The Inbox

Steps added on the fly land in the **Inbox**. Sort them into projects at the weekly review.

## Decisions

Your decisions are listed under the projects, grouped as **To check**, **Open**, **Decided** and **Closed**. See [Decisions](decisions.md).

## Places

A project can belong to a place, or to Anywhere. See [Places and trips](places-and-trips.md).

To start a project, tap the round **+** button on Projects: give it a name and a suit (the place is under **More**). Finished projects are folded away under **Closed**; tap it to open the list.
