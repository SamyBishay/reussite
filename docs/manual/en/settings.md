# Settings

Open **Settings** from Home's menu. It is a plain page with its own heading. Everything you choose here is a setting that stays: an update never resets it.

Each section is folded: only **Appearance** is open to begin with, and a tap on a heading opens another. Inside a section, the few controls most people use are in view and the rest are under **More**. If something under More has been set to a value, More says how many ("More · 2 set"), so nothing set is ever out of sight.

![Settings](shot:settings)

## Language

English or Français. It changes at once.

## Appearance

- **Theme:** System, Light, Dark, or By time of day (you set when light starts and dark starts).
- **Palette** and **Accent colour**, and the **Suit colours**. New people start with the **af Klint** palette. If you chose one before, it stays. Réussite warns you when a colour is hard to read (aim for a contrast of 4.5 to 1).
- **Text size:** Small, Medium or Large.
- **Reduce motion**.
- **Celebrations:** Off, Calm or Lively. **Sound** and **Volume**.
- **Open websites inside Réussite.** Off by default: links open in your browser or the app itself.
- **Export theme** and **Import theme** share a look with a friend.

## Method

The numbers the method runs on, with sensible defaults:

- **Highlight due dates within (days).** Three weeks by default. A due date inside it shows in red. It doesn't make a card urgent: you tick that.
- **Day starts at.** 05:00 by default. A late night still counts as the same day until then.
- **Most work cards per day.** Three by default.
- **Planning time (min).** 15 by default: the time set aside for dealing the day itself.
- **Day ends at.** 19:00 by default. Dealing the day counts the room between its start and this time; each morning's "until" replaces it for that day.
- **Suits:** these are your four suits, the parts of your days that matter to you. Rename them and choose their symbols.
- **Use Places and trips**, then **Places**, their **areas** and **Stays.** On a new Réussite it starts off, and the place at the top of the lists, a card's place, stays and Plan this trip are hidden until you switch it on. See [Places and trips](places-and-trips.md).
- **Use Addresses & people**, and the **address categories.** Also off on a new Réussite. See [Addresses and people](addresses-and-people.md).

A switch that is on because you already have places, stays, addresses or people is greyed, and says why. Nothing you saved is deleted by switching one off: it comes back when you switch it on. If you used Réussite before this change, both are on.
- Times for an **obligation**, the **weekly review day and time**, the **morning check-in** and **closing the day**. **Start the review** is here too, and opens the review on any day, even on a new Réussite where it waits. Notifications use them.

## Tabs

**Show every tab** brings Calendar, Projects and the Matrix into the tab bar now, for good. It only shows while one of them has not arrived yet. A new Réussite starts with Home and Today and the others arrive by themselves; see [Getting started](getting-started.md). If you used Réussite before, you already see them all.

## How Réussite understands you

**On this device** (the default), or **a cloud AI**: **My own key** and **My AI chat app, through the connector**, each with its own controls here. Under the choice, **Is it all linked?** lists sync, the recovery key, the connector, when your chat app last came in and whether your key works; tap a line that is not done to go to what does it. Choosing On this device never switches the connector off. See [AI](ai.md).

## Notifications

Reminders that reach your phone at the right minute, even when Réussite is closed. On an iPhone or iPad, add Réussite to the Home Screen first. Notifications don't work without it.

Switch on **Notify me on this device**, then choose what to be notified about:

- **Time to leave:** before a fixed-time item, with the minutes of notice you choose.
- **Morning check-in** (it says what the day holds and the room it leaves) and **Close the day.**
- **Weekly review.**
- **Decision check.**

By default a notification says only "Start" or "Leave now". You can choose **With titles**. Either way the text is scrambled on your device, and only your device can read it: the relay and the push service see neither. **Send a test notification** checks that it works.

Reminders are renewed each time you open the app. If you don't open it for about two weeks, they run out.

## Calendar

Subscribe to your fixed-time items from a calendar app. Choose **What the feed says**: times only, short names, or short names, places and notes. See [The calendar](calendar.md).

## Data

- **Export a backup** saves a file with your whole deck.
- **Restore a backup** merges a backup into what is on this device. Newer edits here win, and a backup of what was here before is kept.
- **Export an encrypted backup** (when sync is on) opens only on a device that holds your recovery key.
- **Import from Réussite 1** brings your deck over from the first version. You see what will be imported before anything is written.
- **Keep my deck on this device** asks the browser not to clear your deck when space runs low.

If sync is off, Réussite reminds you every two weeks to save a backup file. It is your safety net.
