# Questions for the week of 2 Nov 2026: answered

Each question with the recommendation and the answer. Where the owner said nothing, the recommendation stands.

1. **Takeover margin.** A fixed-time item takes over Start here when the step in progress wouldn't finish `max(5 min, 20 % of its estimate)` before the item's leave time (its time minus its own `lead`). *Why:* short steps get a fixed buffer, and long ones get room to overrun. **Answer: as recommended.**
2. **A planned step that doesn't fit before a fixed item.** It waits for the next gap, and a later step that fits moves up; the planned order is otherwise kept. *Why:* it fills the gap without splitting a step or breaking the order. **Answer: as recommended.**
3. **The four pillars.** The four suits as they are: ♠ Work, ♦ Papers, ♣ Family, ♥ Life (leisure). *Why:* every step already has a suit, so no migration is needed. **Answer: as recommended.**
4. **Free time.** A `dayEnd` setting (default 19:00); each morning's "until" overrides it. *Why:* overcommitment needs an end, and the app only knows the start. **Answer: as recommended.**
5. **Planned steps in the Calendar.** Projected and faded in the in-app Calendar only; not stored as times, and not in the feed. *Why:* it keeps the 23 Oct decision and steps stay floating. **Answer: as recommended.**
6. **The count measure.** Each advancing step adds 1 by default, editable at done. *Why:* simple, and it never counts cards blindly. **Answer: as recommended.**
7. **The morning push.** The existing check-in reminder time, with the new message text. *Why:* the setting exists; only the words change. **Answer: as recommended.**
8. **The `launch` and `still` pushes.** Removed; their stored settings are kept and ignored. *Why:* no start/restart nudges, and invariant 3 forbids dropping what was chosen. **Answer: as recommended.**

**Correction from the owner, applied throughout the plan.** Not every feature has to work without AI. Every step of the psychologist's method must have a no-AI option. With AI on, the workflow can be completely different: a conversation, not the no-AI screens with reworded text.
