# Rule 9: Goals are SMART

**The rule.** A goal is specific, measurable, attainable, relevant and dated. In Réussite it is an outcome in one sentence, a measure (a count towards a target, milestones you tick off, or none) and a start and due date.

**Grade: some support** for setting clear goals and for deciding in advance when and where to act. The SMART checklist as a whole was not tested, and no goal-setting trial in adults with ADHD turned up.

## What the studies found

- A meta-analysis of randomised trials (141 papers, 16,523 people) found that setting a goal has a small positive effect on behaviour (d = .34). Goals worked better when they were difficult, set publicly or shared by a group. These are health and behaviour-change trials, not ADHD. (Epton, Currie and Armitage, 2017.)
- A meta-analysis of 45 articles (5,912 people) found a medium effect of setting goals on physical activity compared with no goal setting (d = .55). (McEwan and colleagues, 2016.)
- A meta-analysis of 94 tests found that deciding in advance when, where and how to act helped people reach goals, with a medium-to-large effect (d = .65). This supports the dated and specific part of SMART, not the acronym. (Gollwitzer and Sheeran, 2006.)
- A review of 35 years of research on goal setting covers the core findings, how goals work and the theory's limits. We read only its abstract, so we quote no number from it. (Locke and Latham, 2002.)

## What they do not show

- No test of the five SMART letters together as a checklist. The research supports parts of it: clear goals, a date, a plan of when and where. "Attainable" pulls against the finding that harder goals did better, so the acronym is not a research result.
- None of these studies is on adults with ADHD.
- The effects are small to medium.
- SMART comes from a 1981 management article, not from research. We could not open it. A paper on the side effects of goal setting, "Goals gone wild" (Ordóñez and colleagues, 2009), exists, but we could not read it, so we say nothing of what it argues.
