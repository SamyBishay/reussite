# Réussite

A planner for people who find starting harder than working. Réussite is the French name of the card game patience, and the word for success.

## Everything you have to do is a card

Type or dictate a plain line: "dentist Friday 14h", "call the bank tomorrow", "done the report". Réussite makes the card, with its day, its time and how long it should take. Each card names its **first move**, the small action that puts you at it ("Open Mail and reply to the agency"), so there is nothing to work out before you begin.

![Home: one box to type in](shot:home)

## Each day, a short hand

In the morning you deal the day: three cards and not thirty, laid out against the clock around what is fixed. Réussite adds up the time, corrected by how long things really take you, and says so when it is more than the day has.

![The morning: the day's hand, against the time you have](shot:checkin)

## One card at a time

Today shows one card, **Start here**. Tap Start and a timer runs. If you are stuck, it asks what is in the way and helps you find a smaller first move. When you finish, the card lands on one of four piles: work, leisure, your own projects, other people.

![Today: the card to start with, and the four piles](shot:today)

## In the evening, the cards you played

You see what you played, pile by pile, and the projects that moved. There are no streaks, no guilt, and no word about what you didn't do.

![The close of the day](shot:close)

## When there is more

- **The Matrix** sorts your cards by urgent and important, so that what matters and has no deadline gets a place every day.
- **Projects** have a goal you can measure, and a bar that only real progress fills.
- **Decisions** have a method of their own: every option, weighed for doing it and for not doing it.
- **A calendar** for what happens at a fixed time. Your own calendar app can follow it.
- **Reminders** on your phone, if you want them.

## Yours, on your device

There is no account, and it works offline. Your list stays on your phone or your computer. If you switch sync on to use two devices, the list is encrypted before it leaves. An AI is optional: your own key, or the chat app you already use. Without one, everything works.

## Where it comes from

Réussite applies a cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) method for adult ADHD. It helps you apply a method. It doesn't replace therapy or care from a professional.

## Start

Open Réussite, type the one thing you have been putting off, and play your first card. It takes two minutes. On an iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
