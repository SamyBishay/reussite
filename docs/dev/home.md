## A line, from input to receipt

1. `submitHome` (`src/app/home.ts`). If the card is asking which of several steps, or how long a step took, the line answers it first.
2. `parseHome` reads the line (below). The result is one of:
   - `changes`: one change-set (`by: 'me'`, so the step gets `by: 'me'`, `src: 'home'`, and its estimate `estBy: 'me'`), with its `intent` (`add`, `standing`, `done`, `move`, `drop`, `note`: a line for a card's note, `several`: more than one thing in the line). `long: true` marks a new card whose words are a sentence (over eight).
   - `act`: the timer, which is no change to the list: `start` a card (`id`), `pause` the one that runs, or `spent` (`id`, `min`): time on a card said after the fact. `act()` in `home.ts` runs it: start puts the card in today's hand (`addToday`), starts its timer and goes to Today; pause pauses the running timer (none: the card says so); spent logs a typed session (`addSession`) as a `duration` turn of the exchange, with its Undo (History takes it back), and toasts "Time saved: …".
   - `pick`: a done, move, drop, note, start or spent matches several open cards, or only nearly matches one ("done the reprot"). The card lists up to five; a tap or a short follow-up ("the bank one") picks, and `choose` reads the line again with `ctx.only` set to the picked card: every lookup of the line then means that card, however it was named, so a name that was only nearly right is taken only after the person's tap.
   - `sheet`: understood, but it needs the task sheet (a repeat the schedule cannot hold). The sheet opens prefilled.
   - `unknown` with a reason (`empty`, `question`, `several`, `memory`, `long`, `nomatch`): goes through `routeUnknown`.
3. A `changes` result goes through `run`: `planChanges`, then `isLowRisk`. **One change that adds a step, ticks one done, or moves one date (`due`, or `due` and `at`) is applied at once.** A line for a card's note (`intent: 'note'`) is also applied at once (`run(…, atOnce)`): they are the person's own words on their own card. Several things in one line (`intent: 'several'`) always open the review. A `long` new card is created at once with no AI; with an AI on (`aiOn`), the line goes to the AI instead (`askHome`), as every sentence did before 15 Nov. The receipt reads "Added “…”", "Done: “…”", "Moved “…”" with **Undo**. Everything else opens the review (`openChangeReview`, with its Undo recorded): a drop, a decision, any memory change, several changes. If the review is closed without applying, the card keeps "n changes · Review" for ten minutes and nothing is written.
4. A step ticked done with no timer running asks, in the card, how long it took (the duration buttons, or a typed "25" or "1h30"; "I didn't time it" leaves no session). With a timer running, its time is saved. The session joins the same Undo.

Typing a line that opens the task sheet writes nothing. Closing the sheet writes nothing; Save creates the step (`openNewTask(prefill, onSaved?)`, a draft in memory).

## The parser's grammar

The parser is pure and deterministic. It reads English and French at once, whatever the app's language, because people mix them and dictation does. An "R:" prefix, or a leading "add", "ajouter", "remind me to", "rappelle-moi de", is dropped. Accents and case are ignored.

| What | English | Français | Becomes |

| Move | `move X to friday`, `push`, `postpone`, `reschedule`, `defer`, `X → friday` | `reporter X à vendredi`, `déplacer`, `repousser`, `décaler`, `X → vendredi` | `task.update` of `due` (and `at`) |
| Drop | `drop X`, `abandon`, `scrap` | `supprimer X`, `abandonner X` | `task.drop`; opens the review |
| A day | `today`, `tonight`, `tomorrow`, `the day after tomorrow`, `in 3 days/weeks/months`, `friday`, `next friday`, `26/10`, `26 oct`, `october 26`, `2026-10-26` | `aujourd'hui`, `ce soir`, `demain`, `après-demain`, `dans 3 jours/semaines/mois`, `vendredi`, `vendredi prochain`, `26/10`, `26 oct`, `le 26 octobre` | `due` |

| A length in words | `for an hour`, `, half an hour`, `for twenty minutes` | `pendant une heure`, `, une demi-heure` | `dur` or `est` |

Rules that are easy to get wrong:

- **A time right after by, before, due, avant, d'ici, jusqu'à or pour (a day may stand between) is a deadline's time (`dueAt`), never an appointment (`at`); the first other time is the appointment.** The line never makes a `slot`: slots come from Plan my day and the step's sheet (`docs/dev/slots.md`).
- **"Nh" is a time when N is 5 or more, or after at, à, vers, from, de; `1h`, `2h30` and anything after for, pendant, about is a length.** `19h`, `7pm` and `14:30` are always times.
- **A weekday on its own is the next one, never today.** `26/10` is day first (month first only when the day is over 12 and the month is not); a date already gone this year is next year's. Short English day names (`mon`, `sat`, `sun`) count only after on, by, next, this, every.
- **A time with no date** is today, or tomorrow if it has already passed today by the day-start rule (`atOnDay`): at 20:00, "call mum 9:00" is tomorrow; at 00:30 with the day starting at 04:00 it is that same morning.
- **A line adds one `task.create` to the Inbox** (`due` only when the text says one). It is not also put on today's plan. Up to 8 words after the date, time and length are taken out it is a plain line; from 9 to 30 it is a sentence (`long: true`: created at once with no AI, sent to the AI with one); over 30 it is a pasted paragraph (`unknown/long`: the AI, or the sheet). The words of a polite opening count towards the 8.
- **Several things in one line.** A semicolon, a new line, "then", "and then", "puis", "et puis", "ensuite" always part two things. A comma or "and"/"et"/"&" parts them only when every piece opens with a verb (`opensWithVerb`, `src/domain/verbs.ts`: a list of English verbs, French infinitives, and the nouns that look like one: "loyer", "dossier") or a done/move/drop word: "call the bank and book the dentist" is two cards, "buy milk and eggs" and "call Anna and Tom" are one. A line that opens with "open", "go to", "log in" (the method's way to write one step: "Open Mail and reply to the agency") is one card, and so is a second half that leans on the first with a pronoun ("… and send it", "-le"). Every piece is read as a line of its own; the result is one change-set only if every piece was read, else `unknown/several`.
- **The suit and the project** (`src/domain/guess.ts`): a project named in the line (its name as whole words, a heading "Thesis: …", or "#thesis") puts the card in it and in its suit; else the suit the person's own cards with the same words went to (`suitHistory`, cards not made on Home; a word must have been seen twice, and the first suit must lead the second by half again); else the suits' own names and descriptions in Settings, matched through a small list of everyday words in both languages ("rent" → money and papers). No guess on a tie or when nothing speaks: the card has no suit, as before.
- **The first move** (`starter`) is written from the title's verb (`suggestFirstMove`, `src/domain/firstMove.ts`, which also serves the first-card screen), through `ctx.firstMove`: the app passes a function that returns the sentence in the app's language ("pay" → "Open the bank app"). No known verb, no first move.
- **The ticks** are read only at the two ends of the line: "urgent: …" or "important: …" before, ", important", "(urgent)", " - urgent", "asap", "!!" after. "An urgent letter" keeps its word.
- **Waited for.** A line opening with "waiting for", "en attente de", "j'attends" makes a waited-for card (`kind: 'event'`) and never a thing to do; a day in the line is its check date (`nudge`). It is never given a suit or a first move.
- **A note** names an open card (a standing obligation too) by the same rules as done; the line after the colon goes on a new line after what the note holds. Applied at once.
- **The timer.** "start X" starts a card only when X names one ("start the report"); with no such card, "start a blog" is a new card. "pause", "stop", "stop the timer", "arrêter le chrono" pause the one that runs. "X took 40 min", "spent 25 minutes on X", "25 min on X", "j'ai passé 1h30 sur X" log a session only when the length reads as nothing but a length and X names a card.
- **Done, move and drop said as facts** ("I paid the rent", "rent is done", "les impôts sont faits", "the photos are for monday", "report tomorrow instead", "forget the photos", "cancel X") act only when the rest names a card; otherwise the line is read as a new card ("cancel the gym membership" with no such card is a thing to do). "done the report and the rent" ticks both when each names a card.
- **A name that is nearly right** ("done the reprot", "fini les impot") is a `pick` offer, never taken: every word must be in the title or one letter off (a word of 4 letters or more; two letters in one of 7; "ph" read as "f", a swap of two letters counts as one). After the tap, `ctx.only` makes the line mean the picked card.
- **Every day or every week on named days, with a time and no other date, is a standing obligation** (`template`, `weekdays`, `at`, the default lead). A repeat without a time, with a gap longer than a week, or with a date opens the sheet with the Repeats field filled. **"until <a day>" after a repeat** (`until`, `till`, `through`, `jusqu'au`; the date right after the word, in any form a date can be written) is the repeat's last day: `until` on a standing obligation ("choir every Tuesday 18:00 until 31 Dec"), `repeat.until` on a repeat the sheet holds. With no repeat in the line, "until Friday" is a due date as before.
- **"at X" after a time stays in the title** as ", X" ("Choir, Hill House 602" at 19:00), unless X starts with an article or is a place you have.
- **Targets of done, move and drop** are open steps that are not standing obligations. A name matches exactly, or by a unique start of whole words ("choir" finds "Choir dues"; "cho" finds nothing), or by the words together anywhere in the title, or by all its words anywhere. Several matches give a `pick`; none gives `unknown/nomatch`, never a guess. If the words also find a standing obligation, the line goes to the AI or the sheet and never to another step that starts the same. A done keyword is a word of its own: "finish the essay" and "terminer le rapport" are new steps.
- **Goes to the AI, or to the sheet if none:** a question (`?`, or what, who, when, why, how, qui, quand, pourquoi, comment…), a statement about someone in memory ("Ibrahim is…", or a note's name and a verb), a line of several things one of which could not be read, and a title over 30 words. With an AI on, a sentence of 9 to 30 words also goes to the AI; with none it is a card (15 Nov; before, every line over eight words and every line of several clauses went to the AI or the sheet).

## The corpus

`src/domain/parse.corpus.ts` is the measure of the mode with no cloud AI: about 350 lines a person types or dictates, in English and French, each with what it should become (`Want`: cards with their fields, a done, move, drop, note, timer, pick, sheet, or unknown with its reason, or several of these). The context is fixed (`CORPUS_CTX`: Saturday 14 November 2026, 10:00 in Paris, a few cards, places and projects, four suits described in words). `parse.corpus.test.ts` reads every line, compares only what the case gives (a card's suit, project and first move only where the line is about them; `null` means there must be none) and prints the score by category.

**To add a line**, put `[line, want]` in the category it belongs to (`plain`, `lead`, `date`, `time`, `deadline`, `length`, `place`, `standing`, `done`, `move`, `drop`, `several`, `long`, `suit`, `project`, `first`, `flag`, `waiting`, `note`, `timer`, `fuzzy`, `other`). A new rule starts here, as lines that fail; then the parser changes until they pass and nothing else breaks.

**Known limits** are the category `limit`: lines the parser reads wrong today, recorded as it reads them, so that a fix flips them (move the line to its category with the right `want`). Today:

- "for" before a day goes with the day ("send Anna the photos she asked for tomorrow" loses its "for").
- A number of minutes is the card's length wherever it stands ("wait 10 minutes before calling again" is a card of 10 minutes).
- One breath with no comma between two things is one card ("call the bank tomorrow pay the rent friday").
- "And" between two things to do makes two cards even when the second leans on the first without a pronoun ("pick up the parcel and bring the receipt").
- A day in the middle of a sentence is the card's day, even when it tells what happened, and leaves a hole in the title ("… the card they blocked on Thursday evening …" is due Thursday, titled "… they blocked evening …").

## The greeting

Two pure things in `src/domain/greeting.ts`, both run on the 30-second clock tick, and one order they read, `dayOrder` (`src/domain/dayOrder.ts`, also what Today's Start here and its list read; `dayOrderOf` in `src/app/model.ts` gathers the data).

**The line** is a hello, chosen when Home opens (`GreetingLine` in `Home.tsx`) from `en.ts`/`fr.ts` (`greetAny…`, and `greetMorning…`, `greetAfternoon…`, `greetEvening…`, `greetNight…` for the hour: morning until noon, afternoon until 18:00, evening until 22:00, night after and before `dayStart`). It says nothing about the day: no fixed item, project, count or list (the owner, 1 Nov). The lines run across registers, places and ages, from "Hwæt!" and "Good morrow" to "Wazzaaap." and a letter's "Veuillez agréer… mes salutations distinguées", with "Hello." among them; each language has its own lines under the same keys (the French are not translations). They ask for nothing (no question mark, no "type", "add", "move" or "tick off"), never mention what was missed or carried, and never say database, record, entry or field (a unit test scans both languages). `pickGreeting` returns the candidate shown longest ago (never shown first), in an order seeded by the day, so a line is not repeated within a day while another is eligible; the shown keys are in `localStorage` under the day (`reussite.greet.<day>`, `src/app/greetingShown.ts`, try/catch, older days dropped), one more each time Home mounts. It is the same with an AI on: no call is made.

**The card** is `greeting()` (`greetingNow` in `src/app/model.ts`), and it is only ever one of two, or none. **Start here, the running step, the clock with Pause and tomorrow's first step are Today's alone** (`FirstCard` in `Today.tsx`; no other tab and not Home carries a Start here bar or a running bar; "Still on…?" is the one banner that stays on every tab, because it fixes data). In this order, the first that applies:

1. **A running timer, or a closed day**: no card, only the line.
2. **Nothing on today's list yet** (a fixed-time item that is first does not count as a list): Deal the day (with an AI on, it opens the morning proposal, `docs/dev/morning.md`, and Plan it myself opens the ritual; with the connector on and no key, a second button opens the AI chat).
3. **Everything on it done** (the list holds cards and nothing is left to start): a line saying so, with Close the day.
4. **Otherwise** (the plan is open, something is left to start): no card; the person starts it on Today.

## The card and the nudge

The card shows, in this order of priority, one of:

1. What an open exchange has to say or ask: which step (`pick`), how long a step took (`duration`), "n changes · Review" (`review`), or an AI's answer (`answer`). It lives in memory and lapses after ten minutes; only what was applied is in the exchange.
2. Otherwise **one nudge**, the first of: the recovery key not saved (sync on); a decision due for checking; yesterday's open steps (the nudge's button opens `CarriedSheet`, one card at a time, not a bulk carry); the review day. Nothing if none.

## Exchanges and turns

An exchange (`exchanges` collection, synced) is written when a change-set is applied, or when an AI answers. It holds `at`, `text`, `turns`, `changes` (the change-set ids applied), `undo` (change-set id → `UndoDoc[]`), `undone` and `src`.

- `src`: `home`, `link` (a one-tap link opened where the list is) or `inbox` (a link sent through the relay). A `link` or `inbox` exchange is always its own: Home never continues it, and a Home line a minute later does not join it. Its text is the change-set's note, else "Changes from an AI's link".
- **Turn `kind`**: `add`, `standing`, `done`, `move`, `drop` (the local parser applied it; `reply` is the step's title, `n` the changes written), `changes` (any other applied change-set), `answer` (an AI answered, nothing changed), `duration` (how long a ticked step took). The receipt is built from the turns in the app's language when read, not stored.
- **Follow-ups.** A line within ten minutes of the last turn joins the newest exchange of this device, unless every change in it was undone, so "3 changes · Undo" takes back all three. After ten minutes Home starts clean: the receipt goes, and History keeps the exchange.
- **Never one another device wrote to.** A follow-up joins an exchange only if every field of it was last written by this device (its clocks carry this device's node). `changes` and `undo` merge whole, so two devices adding turns to one exchange before syncing would lose one change-set and its Undo.
- **Pruning.** Exchanges older than 90 days (by their last turn) are emptied (text, turns, changes, undo, undone) and then tombstoned, when the app starts. History lists the last 90 days, newest first.

## Undo

One pure module, `src/ai/undo.ts`; the relay's connector uses it too. In the app, `recordWrites(fn)` in `store.ts` notes the local `createDoc`, `update` and `removeDoc` made while `fn` runs (one run at a time; sync's `putMerged` is never noted) and gives the `UndoDoc[]`; `applyUndo` reverts them.

- Undo reverts all of an exchange's change-sets, newest first, in one go.
- **"Left alone."** A field is put back only if it still holds what the change-set wrote. A field edited since stays, and the toast says how many ("Undone. 2 edits you made since were left alone.").
- **A step the change-set made** is tombstoned (never hard-deleted) only if no field of it has a clock newer than the change-set's newest write (`UndoDoc.at`) and nothing made since refers to it (a session on the step, a step in a project). Otherwise it stays and counts as left alone.
- **A timer the change-set stopped** is started again only within ten minutes (`FRESH_MS`). After that `started` stays empty and the session that saved its time stays.
- **A document the change-set removed** is not brought back: a tombstone has no way back.
- **In History, past ten minutes**, Undo asks first.
- **Forget** (memory) is never recorded for Undo. Forgetting a line or note also goes through the exchanges: one whose Undo holds that note loses those records, and its text and every turn's text and reply are emptied (History shows "Forgotten from Memory").

## The AI route and "outside"

`routeUnknown`: without an AI set up, the task sheet opens prefilled with what was read. With one (`aiOn`), `askHome` sends the line with the earlier turns of the open exchange (the last six) and the memory notes it names. The reply is a change-set:

- Changes go through `run`, like a typed line: one low-risk change at once, else the review.
- No changes: the answer is the change-set's `note`, shown on the card and kept as an `answer` turn, so History lists it and a follow-up within ten minutes has the context.
- **`outside: true`** is an optional field of the envelope that only Home's prompt asks for: the request is not about the list, the memory or the method, and the reply is one line pointing to their usual AI. The card then shows that line alone, with no button (the screens no longer offer Ask any AI). It is read when there are no changes, and it is **not** in `docs/CHANGESET.md`: an outside AI is never told about it, and the contract stays version 1.
- If the AI fails, or the monthly limit is reached, the card says one plain sentence with **Add as a card**, which opens the card's form prefilled. Cancel in the preview leaves the text in the input. Nothing is written.

"Show me what's sent" applies as everywhere, and lists the memory notes sent (private notes are never among them).

## Spend and the limit

Per device and month, in `local` (`aiUsage.YYYY-MM`; never synced): calls, tokens, and dollars for the calls that have a price (`src/ai/ask.ts`, `src/ai/prices.ts`).

- Dollars are OpenRouter's `cost` (in every reply), or, for Anthropic's own API, the table in `prices.ts`: Haiku 5.5 $0.10 in / $0.50 out, Sonnet 5.5 $2 / $10, Opus 5.5 $4 / $20 per million tokens; cache reads $0.01 / $0.20 / $0.20; five-minute cache writes 1.25 times the input price. Haiku is not priced above 100K prompt tokens. Any other provider or model shows tokens only: no price is guessed. The table is dated in the file; update it when the prices change.
- `aiMonthlyCap` (US dollars, default none, Settings → How Réussite understands you → My own key → More): when this month's dollars reach it, every AI call stops with the `cap` error before the preview, so nothing is shown or sent. Only calls with a dollar figure count toward it, and the settings page says so.
- My own key (Settings → How Réussite understands you) shows under More the month's calls and tokens, the dollars so far, and a note that dollars count only priced calls.

## Memory in a request

Every feature's prompt does this (Home, command box, plan, break it down, decisions, stuck, review). The system prompt is two parts: a stable one (intro, method, the profile note, the language line; no date) and the feature's own. The profile (all non-private profile notes) is in the stable part, so its bytes are the same in every feature. In the user message, `pickNotes` adds the notes the message, the steps sent or their projects name, within `memoryBudget` (3,000 tokens, a setting; the profile counts first): whole words of a name or alias, most recently updated first; a note that does not fit is skipped and a smaller one after it may still go. **A private note is never picked.** The note format (`noteHead`, `noteBlock`) is one function in `src/domain/memory.ts`, shared with the connector's `read_memory`.

## The morning ritual (plan 2 Nov, S2)

`src/flows/checkin.tsx`, one question per screen: yesterday (a line from `cardsOn`/`minutesOn`, then `CarriedFlow`: each open step on its own card, then `MissedFlow`: each fixed item of yesterday whose time passed, Move or Remove; both in `src/app/CarryUi.tsx`), the shape (a kind of day, **from** and **until**), the four pillars, the plan, and the first step alone. `days.shape` is `{start, end?, preset}`: `end` is optional and additive (no migration); `dayOrderOf` (`src/app/model.ts`) reads it, so a day's `end` is its "until" (else `settings.dayEnd`) and nothing is placed before its `start`. The start defaults to now to the next 5 minutes (not before `dayStart`); `launchDefaults` is still stored and no longer read. The pillars are the four suits (`pillarsOf`, `PILLARS` in `src/domain/planner.ts`): each row is the planner's steps of that suit, with Swap and Skip; an empty pillar says so and offers Add one (a step of that suit, placed like a swapped-in one). The plan is laid out with `dayOrder` over the screen's own items (fixed items at their time to leave, a standing obligation at its time, the steps in the gaps), and `dayLoad` gives "x of y free" and the minutes over; the ratio is already in `planEst`. Go writes the plan, `first`, `shape` and `load`, then shows the first item of `dayOrderOf` alone with Start (a fixed item that has taken over comes first). Launch points no longer exist in the planner (`Plan` has no `launches`), Today, Settings or the close; `days.launches` is kept as stored and the change-set still accepts it.

## Carried and missed, one at a time (plan 2 Nov, S3)

`src/app/CarryUi.tsx` holds the cards; there is no bulk carry (`carryOver` is gone). `carriedOn(k)` (`src/app/model.ts`) is yesterday's open steps (`yesterdayOpen`) without the fixed-time items that passed; `missedOn(k)` is `dayOrder(...).missed` over a day's fixed items (`fixedOnDay`), read at the clock and not narrowed by the area chip, so for yesterday it is every fixed item not done. A **CarriedCard** answers with existing actions only: Today is `carry` (counts, and keeps the step on yesterday's list in the ritual, which writes the day's list itself); Another day is `planOn`; Drop it is `dropTask` and `droppedLine` ("Unlocked: …") on a second tap; Make it a decision is `planOn` on today, `kind: 'decision'`, then the decision flow; Already done is `finishTask` for yesterday. A **MissedCard** answers Move with `moveFixed` (a day and a time), Remove with `dropTask` (`skipThisTime` for a repeating one: this time only), Already done with `finishTask`. Nothing writes until a button is tapped. `MissedNow` shows today's first passed item on Home and Today; Today's `carry` banner and the bell's nudge open `openCarried()`. No count of either appears in the greeting.

- **Start here** (`FirstCard` in `src/views/Today.tsx`): the main row is Start or Continue (`timerLabel`), or the clock, Pause and Played while it runs, and **Played** (`openDone`, which asks how long when no time is logged; "Already played" is gone from here). The quiet row is **Note** (`openNote`: the card's `NoteField` alone and Save, `src/app/taskSheet.tsx`), **Move** (`openMove`) and **Drop** (`DropButton`); a running card has Note only. **I'm stuck** is on the face only when the card was paused (`hasLogged`, or a `resume` line) or carried over (`carried > 0`). ## Today, the period navigator, decisions and the Calendar's scroll (1 Nov)

- **`PeriodNav`** (`src/app/PeriodNav.tsx`) is the one ‹ label › navigator: Today's day (`Navigator` in `Today.tsx`, with the label as a native date input) and the Calendar's bar (`Calendar.tsx`). A three-column grid (`44px 1fr 44px`), one compact row: ‹ and › are in fixed columns at the two ends and never move; between them a flex row holds the label (ellipsised) and, when away, the **↩ Today** chip (`data-testid="back-today"`, `aria-label` Back to today, 44px tall to tap, the arrow alone under 360px). No line is reserved and there is no sub line: at today the label is Today, away it is Tomorrow, Yesterday or the day in its short form ("Wed 4 Nov"). Test ids kept: `prev-day`, `next-day`, `day-label` (Today), `cal-prev`, `cal-next`, `cal-title`. The e2e `periodnav.spec.ts` compares the arrows' boxes across today, tomorrow, a far date and the Calendar's views at 320 and 390, and taps › three times at one spot.
- **Today's rhythm**: Today's root is `.todayview`, a column with `--s3` between the navigator, the banners, Start here, the carry card and the list's block; children set no margins of their own (`.todayview>*`). On a phone (under 768px) the same blocks are 8px apart and condensed so that Start here is whole in view on 375×667 on a Sunday with the review box (`tests/e2e/todayfit.spec.ts`): the suit piles are 30px cards, the banners' text and button share a row, Start here has 12px of padding, and under 700px of height its hint line is hidden. The top of the app has no cards-count line any more; the minutes timed are in the masthead (`Mast`, `.mast-timed`), between the date and the place pill. The meta line's dots are hidden by CSS (the gap says it), so a wrapped line never starts with one.
- **Decisions** (`src/views/Decisions.tsx`) are on the Matrix under the four boxes, above the obligations and All cards folds; Projects has none (a project's card still lists its linked decisions).
- **The Calendar's scroll**: `CalendarGrid.tsx` no longer uses Schedule-X's scroll-controller. `place()` measures the scroll viewport (`.sx__view-container`) and the sticky date header and sets `scrollTop` from `gridScrollTop` (`src/domain/calScroll.ts`): the time now in the middle of the visible hours when today is among the shown days; otherwise the first timed event of the shown days with an hour above it, or 07:30 at the top. `settle()` repeats it over ~24 animation frames after a view or date change, a new grid height (a `ResizeObserver` on the host, whose height is flexible: `.app[data-view="calendar"]` is one screen tall, `html.home-lock` stops the page scrolling, `.calview` is a flex column and `.sxhost` takes what the bar and the lines under the grid leave) or a new calendar, and stops as soon as the person scrolls or touches the grid. The Log is a mode of the Calendar's bar and scrolls inside `.calscroll`, with the footer at its end.
