# The Réussite manual

Réussite is a planner for adults with ADHD. It is built on a cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) method, and it plays like the card game patience: everything you have to do is a card, each day is a short hand, and each card you play lands on one of four piles. It helps with the hard part: **starting**.

Réussite helps you apply a method. It doesn't replace therapy or care from a professional.

These pages are short and written in plain words. You can read them in any order.

## About the "Research says" and "Practice says" lines

Each rule of the method has two short lines. **Research says** is what studies show, in one sentence, with a link to the page that gives the studies, what they do not show and where to read them. **Practice says** is what the method's practice holds. Many rules have only practice behind them, and the research pages say so. Nobody has tested this app.
