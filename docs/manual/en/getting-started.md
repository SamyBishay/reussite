# Getting started

## Install it

Réussite is a web app. Nothing to download from a store.

- **iPhone or iPad:** open the address in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open Réussite from there. Notifications only work from the Home Screen.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome. Tap the menu, then Install app.
- **Computer:** open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Chrome and Edge show an install icon in the address bar.

It works offline. Your list lives on your device.

## Setting it up

The first time you open it on a new device, Réussite asks for two things and no more.

![The first screen: language](shot:onboarding)

1. **Your language.** It starts in your phone's language.
2. **Four kinds of time in your days**, already filled in: **Work** (job or studies, bureaucracy, administrative paperwork), **Leisure** (fun, relaxation, consumption, hobbies, outings), **Personal projects** (side projects, chores, research) and **Interpersonal obligations** (functions and services for family and friends). Change a name or a description if another fits you better, or keep them. Then **Start**.

That is all: you land on Home. **Skip, use defaults** on the first screen does the same without asking. Everything else is for later and has a sensible default: no AI, no sync, no reminders, no calendar link, and your place is "Anywhere" (add your places from the place menu or Settings). The bell on Home lists these as **Next steps**: an AI, sync (and saving the recovery key), reminders on this device, the calendar link and Addresses & people. Each line opens its section in Settings, a line goes by itself once it is done, and **Hide this** removes the list.

**An AI, later.** Settings → AI → **Set up with an AI…** (or the **Set up an AI** line) opens the same guided screens in a sheet, with three ways:

- **AI inside the app (a key).** Plan the day and the Sunday review are a conversation inside the app. It costs a few cents a month, on your own key. Make an account with OpenRouter (one key reaches many models) or Anthropic (a key for its own models), create a key, paste it; the screen shows the steps. The key stays on this device. You tell the AI about your days and it suggests the four kinds of time, your places and how you want an AI to work with you.
- **Your AI chat app, through the connector.** (Also Settings → Connector → **Connect my AI chat app, step by step**.) You plan your day by talking to your AI chat app: it reads and edits your list, with your yes. It needs sync, and an app that accepts connectors of the MCP kind. The steps switch on sync and show your recovery key to keep (with one **I've saved it** tap before going on), switch on the connector, show the address to add and the pairing code, with short steps for the app, and check that it has come in.
- **Any chatbot.** For a chatbot that can't be connected: Réussite gives you a message to copy into a new chat, and you paste the chat's summary back. Nothing is connected.

Whichever way the AI's suggestion comes back, it is on the review screen, with a tick for each line. Untick what you don't want, or go back and tell the AI what to change. Nothing is written before you tick.

If you already use Réussite on another device, choose **Restore with a recovery key** on the first screen and enter your recovery key. Your places and settings come from the other device, and nothing from this one overwrites them. See [Sync and recovering a lost device](sync-and-recovery.md).

## Your first day

When nothing is planned yet, the bell on Home lists **Plan the day**; it starts the morning check-in. See [The daily loop](daily-loop.md).

Starting is the hard part. Every step in Réussite has a small first action, so the jump from not doing to doing is as small as it can be.
