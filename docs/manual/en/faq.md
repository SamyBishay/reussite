# Questions people ask

## Is this therapy?

No. Réussite helps you apply a method. It doesn't replace therapy or care from a professional.

## Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts. Your list is on your device.

## Do I need an AI?

No. It is optional. Without one, you can still use **Ask any AI** to copy a question into any chatbot, and bring its answer back with **Paste the reply**. See [AI](ai.md).

## Does it work offline?

Yes. The app and the manual's text work offline. A screenshot appears offline once you've seen it online.

## It says "This list was saved by a newer version."

One of your devices has a newer version. This device shows your list, but doesn't edit, until you tap **Reload**. Nothing is lost.

## Nothing happened when I typed on Home.

Réussite asks when a line is unclear. It may be asking which step you mean. If no AI is set up, a line the app can't read opens the step form already filled in. Try a verb: "call the bank tomorrow", "done the report".

## I can't start a step.

Tap **I'm stuck**. Pick the friction, do just the starter, and if it still won't start, write a smaller one. See [The daily loop](daily-loop.md).

## A step keeps carrying over.

A step carried three times or more is too big, or hides a decision. Tap **Shrink it** or **It's a decision** in the check-in.

## I forgot to start the timer.

Tap **I started earlier** and say when you began. Or mark the step done and pick how long it took.

## Where did the "start" times go in my calendar?

A calendar is for what is at a time and what is on a day. Launch points stay in Today and in notifications. The calendar shows only fixed-time items.

## Why is only one step shown when I added five?

Steps that wait for another are one card: the first, with "+4 after". Tap it to see the others. When the first is done, the next takes its place. If you'd rather have them separate, choose **In any order** when you break a step down, or empty "Waits for" on a step.

## Where did my standing items go from the matrix?

They are in the folded **Obligations** line under the grid. A time places them, not a quadrant.

## I lost my phone.

See [Sync and recovering a lost device](sync-and-recovery.md).

## I lost my recovery key.

If another device still has your list, open Settings → Sync & devices there and tap **Show recovery key**. If every device and the key are gone, the list can't be recovered. If you saved a backup file, you can restore it.

## Notifications don't arrive on my iPhone.

Add Réussite to the Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen), open it from there, and switch notifications on in Settings. Also check that you haven't blocked them in the phone's settings.

## Can someone else read my list?

Not without your recovery key. The relay sees only scrambled data, unless you switch the connector on. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

## How do I bring my list from Réussite 1?

Settings → Data → **Import from Réussite 1**. You see what will be imported before anything is written.

## Something broke.

Settings → About → **Copy diagnostics**, and send the report to a friend who can help. It never includes your steps, notes, names, places or keys.
