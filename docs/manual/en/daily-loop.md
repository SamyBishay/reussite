# The daily loop

The loop is short: deal the day, start the first card, close the day. Once a week there is a review, and on a new Réussite it waits until your deck has something to look at.

Réussite uses the words of the card game it is named after. A to-do is a **card**. Your cards are the **deck**. The cards for today are **today's hand**. You **deal** the day, and a card you finish is **played**.

## Deal the day

Open Home and tap the bell, or tap **Deal the day** wherever it is offered (Home when the day has no hand yet, the round **+** on Today, Start here when nothing is open). A notification at your morning check-in time opens it too: it says what the day holds and the room it leaves ("Morning. Today has the 10:00 dentist and room for about 3 h. Deal it?").

### On a new Réussite: the quick deal

A new Réussite deals the day on one screen. You see up to three cards from your deck, and one more for leisure or people. Each has **Keep** (on by default) and **Swap** (it brings the next card in its place). Turn **Keep** off to leave a card out. Then tap **Deal**, and today's hand is made.

If you want the longer way, **The full morning ritual** is one tap away. If your deck has nothing to deal yet, the screen says so.

### The full morning ritual

This is the way Réussite deals the day if you already used it before this change, or if you tap **The full morning ritual**. It is a short ritual, one question per screen. With an AI set up, it opens a proposal instead (see **Deal the day with an AI** below), and the ritual is one tap away.

1. **Yesterday.** One line of what was played. Then each card left open is asked about on its own, one at a time: **Today**, **Another day** (tomorrow, one of the next days, or **No date**: it leaves the day and its due date and stays in your deck, not dropped), **Drop it** (a second tap confirms; it says what it unlocked) or **Make it a decision** (it opens the decision with the card). If it was played and not marked, **Already played** asks how long it took. Nothing moves until you answer. A fixed-time item from yesterday whose time passed is asked about the same way, **Move** (to another day or time) or **Remove**. Réussite says nothing about what didn't happen, and never counts it.
2. **Today's shape.** What kind of day is it (a usual day, afternoon only, an hour or less, a rest day)? **From when** and **until when**: the start begins at the next five minutes, and the end at the day's end (Settings → Method, 19:00 by default); this morning's answer stands in for that setting, today only. What is fixed today? If one of your places has areas, **Where will you be?** lets you pick one for the morning and one for the afternoon (see [Places and trips](places-and-trips.md)).
3. **The four suits.** One row for each of your suits, filled in by the method. Keep each one, **Swap** it for another of the same weight, or **Skip** it (**Keep it** brings it back). A row with nothing in it says so in one line ("No Leisure card today. Fine, or add one?", with your own suit's name) and offers **Add one**.
4. **Today's hand.** The cards are laid out against the clock, with the time each is expected to start, between your fixed times. Under the list: "3 h 10 of 4 h 30 free", the cards' estimates (adjusted by your pace) next to the time you have between the start and the end of the day, minus what is fixed. When there is more than room, it says by how much ("About 50 min more than the time you have.") and **Cut one** takes you to the cutting. Tap **Swap** to pick something of the same weight, or **Cut** to take something off; it stays in your deck for another day. Tap **Go with this** when you are happy.
5. **The first card alone.** Its first move, its estimate and **Start**. **Not yet** closes the ritual without starting the timer.

![Deal the day, by the method's rules](shot:checkin)

What the hand contains:

- Cards that are fixed in time come first, with "Leave at…".
- Then the **Now** cards for the place you're in.
- Then exactly one **Protect** card.
- Then **Quick** cards, if there is room.
- At most three work cards (you can change the limit in Settings → Method).
- One obligation, with a time: leisure, going out or seeing people.
- If you said where you'll be in the afternoon, the cards of that area come together at the end of the hand, and the morning's area first. The hand shows "Afternoon in Heliopolis" on them.
- A little time for the planning itself.

Réussite adds up the estimates and shows what the day will probably take for you.

There are no launch points or restart reminders: cards float between your fixed times, and the order is the one Today follows (see Start here below).

### Deal the day with an AI

When an AI is set up (Settings → How Réussite understands you, **My own key**, with a key on this device), **Deal the day** on Home and the morning notification open a proposal instead of the questions. The AI proposes and you choose; it does not ask you open questions. Before the first one, Réussite shows you what will be sent, as always.

You see your day in five parts, each item with **Skip** and, when there is something else to put in its place, **Swap**:

1. **What has to happen today.** Things at a fixed time (they stay, they are not a choice), cards that are due or urgent, cards from yesterday you still have, and the things you are waiting for that are due to be checked.
2. **Your main goals.** The one or two projects to move today, and for each the card to start with. **Swap** changes the card, or the project.
3. **One for each suit.** What the AI would put in each suit, under the names you gave them ("For Interpersonal obligations: call your mother, 15 min"). When your deck has nothing for a suit, it may write a new card; it is marked **New**.
4. **Where you are.** Given your place today, the cards that belong there.
5. **The day, against the time you have.** "3 h 10 of 4 h 30 free", updated as you skip and swap, and the card to start with. When there is more than the time, it says by how much and leaves out what it would cut first, struck out; **Keep it** brings one back.

A skipped card is only left off today's hand; it stays in your deck. To change the proposal in your own words, type in the box ("lighter on work, add a walk") and tap **Adjust**, or tap **Too much, cut it** or **Room for one more**. What you skipped is not offered again.

**Deal the hand** shows the changes the way every AI change is shown, with a tick for each: the new cards, the broken-down ones, and the day's hand with its first card. Nothing is written before you tick. Undo is on Home's receipt and in History. If the AI is unreachable you still get the five parts, made by the rules and without its wording, with a way to the ritual. **Deal it myself** opens the ritual at any time. With no AI set up, nothing changes.

If you use the connector and have no key, Réussite makes no AI call itself. Instead **Deal it in your AI chat** copies a short sentence ("Deal my day with Réussite") to say in a new chat in your AI chat app, which then reads and edits your deck, with your yes.

**Research says:** CBT programmes that teach planning help adults with ADHD; a short hand with a small first move has not been tested on its own. [See the evidence](research-5.md).

**Practice says:** a short hand with a small first move is easier to start than a long one. A day dealt in a few minutes beats a day planned for an hour.

## Deadlines, appointments and slots

A card can carry three different things, each in its own field in the card's sheet:

- **Due** is a deadline, a day and, if you like, a time: it must be played by then. It never goes in the calendar and never takes the card out of your deck. After the time has passed today the card stays on Today and says “was due 17:00”, in the ordinary colour. After the day it says “carried over”, not “late”.
- **At** is an appointment, something that happens at that time: a call, a visit, a lunch. It goes in the calendar and the reminders. If its time passes and it is not played, it stays on Today marked **missed**, and a small card offers **Move** or **Remove**.
- **Slot** is time you set aside to work on the card: a day, a start and a length. Dealing your day gives each card in the hand a slot. It can move, there is only one per card, and it is not an appointment. When it passes and the card is still open, the card says “slot passed” with **Re-slot**: that opens a proposed new time, and nothing moves until you confirm. The sheet keeps a short log of where the slot has been.

Typed on Home, “pay the rent by 17:00” or “avant 17h” sets a deadline time; a bare time, “lunch Monday 19:00”, sets an appointment. Slots come only from dealing the day or the card's sheet.

## Start here

Today shows the first card of your hand under **Start here**. It shows the card's first move: the smallest first action. The face has few buttons, on purpose.

- **Start the timer** begins the timer. It reads **Continue** once the card has time logged, and **Just start the timer** when the card has an app or a link (the link's own button opens it and starts the timer too).
- **Played** plays the card. If you did not time it, it asks how long it took. This is also how you play a card you finished earlier.
- Under them, smaller: **Note** (opens the card's note alone), **Move** and **Drop it** (a second tap confirms; the card waits in the Cemetery).
- **I'm stuck** is on the face only when you paused the card or it came over from yesterday. Otherwise it is in the card's sheet.

**Tap the card** to open its sheet. It has the rest: **Swap**, **I started earlier** (forgot to start the timer? two taps), **I'm stuck**, and everything about the card.

**Move** opens one sheet for every kind of move: the four boxes (Now, Protect, Quick, Later), then **Plan it on…** with Tomorrow and the next five days, and **Take off today** when the card is in today's hand. Moving a card to tomorrow is two taps, without leaving the screen.

Start here is always there: with nothing open in today's hand, it shows the card your deck would pick next, with **Start the timer**, **Put it first** and **Choose from your deck**.

Today's top is kept short, so that Start here shows whole on a small phone, even on a Sunday with the review box above it. The date is in the top bar, with the time you have timed so far today beside it, small (left out when there is no room). Under it the day changer is one row: **‹ Today ›**. The arrows never move, so you can tap the same spot again and again to go a day at a time. Away from today the label is the day (“Wed 4 Nov”) and a small **↩ Today** chip beside it brings you back; tap the label to pick any date.

Today's hand is the day's cards, and that includes the things at a fixed time: the dentist at 15:00 is a card in the hand, with its time, and the same card is on the calendar. A change in one is a change in the other.

![Today: the first card and the rest of the hand](shot:today)

**The round + is Today's one way to add.** Its sheet makes a new card for today. On Today it also offers **From your deck** (a card you already have), **Leisure & people** and, when your hand has cards but is not dealt yet, **Deal the day**.

**The deck fold.** On a new Réussite, under today's hand there is a fold, **The deck · 12 cards**: it opens your whole deck, the same list as the Matrix's All cards, which arrives later (see [Getting started](getting-started.md)). It counts open and played cards, not standing items, events or dropped cards.

**Start here follows the clock.** It is the first card that fits before your next fixed time, in the order of your hand. A card that does not fit the gap waits for the next one, and a shorter card further down moves up; a card is never cut in two. When the next thing at a fixed time gets close, it takes over: Start here becomes that item, with its time and when to leave. That happens when the card you are on (what is left of it if the timer is running, or its estimate if not), plus a margin of a fifth of its length and at least 5 minutes, would run into the time you need to leave. A card with no estimate counts as 15 minutes. Something at a fixed time whose time has passed leaves the order, and Home and Today show one small card for it, "“Call the bank” was at 10:00", with **Move** (to another day or time) and **Remove** (a second tap confirms; a repeating one skips this time). **Already played** is there too. It is never red, and it is asked one at a time. Today's card and hand follow this one order. Today's hand shows when each card is expected to start ("≈10:40"); that is a projection that moves with the clock, not a promise. Dragging a card by its grip still sets the order of your hand. Things at a fixed time keep their time, wherever you drop them.

**Played cards fold.** Under the hand, the cards you played are gathered in a row, "3 played ▸". Tap it, or press Enter on it, to see them; they are closed by default. Projects fold their played cards the same way.

Adding a card by hand to today puts it right after the card you are timing, or before the first one not played yet. It doesn't wait at the end behind the whole hand.

You can drag a card by its grip to put the hand in another order. The arrows still work. When a card has areas, the hand is grouped under the area's name.

When you play a card that others were waiting for, the next one takes its place right after it in the hand.

If you gave something you are waiting for a day to be asked about, a **Check: …** row appears on Today from that day, and stays until you answer. **It happened** asks which day and frees the cards behind it. **Still waiting** asks again in 3 days, in a week or on a day you pick. **Drop it** gives up waiting. It is never a card in the hand, and with no day it never comes by itself.

## Pause and stuck

Tap **Pause** to stop: one tap, nothing to answer. A card with time already logged offers **Continue**, not Start: the clock picks up from the total, and each run stays its own line in the card's sessions. Playing such a card never asks how long it took. The time so far is saved, and the card keeps its "where I stopped" note.

Tap **I'm stuck** when it won't start. Réussite asks which friction it is:

- **Can't start.** It's there and nothing happens.
- **Stopped and can't get back in.** There was a break and now it won't restart.
- **Drifted.** Attention went elsewhere.

Then it shows the first move, or helps you write a smaller one. If the same card stalls twice, it probably hides a decision or missing information. Réussite asks you to turn it into a question to someone, or a decision.

**Research says:** We found no test of what to do when you are stuck; simple if-then plans do help people start. [See the evidence](research-5.md).

**Practice says:** when you are stuck, don't deal the day again. Find the friction and make the first action smaller.

## Break it down

A card over 30 minutes is too big. Tap **Break it down**: it asks for the first action (the app or place, then what to do there), its first move, a time, and what comes next. It then asks whether the cards go **In this order** or **In any order**. In this order, each card waits for the one before, and they stay together as one card: you see the first, with "+3 after" under it that unfolds the rest. In any order, they are separate cards. See [Timing and estimates](timing-and-estimates.md) for the first-move test.

## Close the day

Tap **Close the day**. Your cards are dealt out one by one, then you see the day's summary. Under Settings → Appearance you can switch the celebration off, calm or lively; with it off you go straight to the summary.

![The close of the day: the suits played, the bar that moved, tomorrow's first card](shot:close)

The summary has:

- The cards you played and the time you logged.
- **One line for your suits**, such as "♠ 2 · ♦ 1 · ♥ 1": the symbol of each suit you played in, with how many cards. A suit you didn't play in is left out.
- **The bars that moved today.** A project's bar moves when a card that moves its goal was played today, or a milestone was ticked today. Each shows its bar and what today added ("4 of 12 pages · +2 today"). Cards alone never move a bar, and cards still open count for nothing and are not mentioned.
- **Tomorrow's first card.** Pick one from your deck, or write a new one; the summary says "Tomorrow starts with: …", and, if you like, when to start it.

Réussite states facts ("4 cards, 2 h 10"). It never praises your character, and it has no streaks.

## The weekly review

On a new Réussite the review waits. It starts once your deck is a week old and holds ten cards, or once you have done one (**Settings → Method → Start the review** opens it on any day). Until then there is no review box on Today or the Matrix, no line behind the bell and no notification. If you already used Réussite before this change, nothing is different: once a week (Sunday by default), Today and the [Matrix](matrix.md) show a **Start the review** box at the top, and the bell lists it.

The review takes about 45 minutes, one question at a time:

1. What changed between the quadrants?
2. What no longer matters?
3. Sort the inbox into projects.
4. Give each project a concrete next card and a SMART goal.
5. Pick the week's Protect cards and their days.
6. Name the week's obligations: people, going out, leisure.
7. Queue the decisions to make, with dates.
8. Check decisions whose date has come.

It ends with how your estimates compared with reality.

### The goals

After the next cards, each active project gets two screens.

- **Is this goal still right?** You see the goal in one sentence, its bar and its dates. **Still right** goes on. **Edit the goal** opens the goal editor in the screen. **Close it** asks whether the goal was **Reached** or you want to **Let it go**; either way the project is closed (you can reopen it in Projects), and its cards are not asked.
- **Which cards move this goal?** Each open card of the project, with **Moves the goal? Yes / No**. The buttons show the answer the card already has, so a card you answered last week stays as you left it; one you never answered says "Not said". Only the cards you say Yes to fill the bar, once they are played.

With an AI set up, **Start the review** (on the review day, from Home's bell, Today or the Matrix, and in Settings → Method as **Review the goals with the AI**) opens a short conversation about the goals instead, one project at a time. For each project the AI checks the goal against SMART (the sentence, the measure and the dates), tells you for each open card whether it belongs to the goal and whether it moves it, and proposes changes: a clearer sentence, a measure, other dates, cards to drop or to add. You keep or **Skip** each proposal, flip any card's **Yes / No** with a tap, and can say what to change ("make the goal smaller"). After the last project, **See the changes** shows one list on the review screen: tick what you want and nothing is written before that. **The rest of the review** then goes on with the usual questions, and **Go through the goals myself** opens the review by hand at any point. Without an AI nothing changes. If you use the connector and have no key, **Review in your AI chat** copies a sentence to say to your AI chat app.

**Research says:** People who plan their time tend to do better and feel better, but that is a correlation, and a weekly review has not been tested on its own. [See the evidence](research-method.md).

**Practice says:** a regular look at everything keeps the daily hand small.
