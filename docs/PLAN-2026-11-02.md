# Réussite: the plan for the week of 2 Nov 2026

**Why.** The app stores what the method produces but doesn't run the process; the old today skill did. This week makes the app run the day: a planning message, a morning ritual, one order for the day, a greeting, a close and a Sunday review. Answers to the questions are in `docs/PLAN-questions.md`; brief edits in `docs/BRIEF-changes.md`.

**The AI rule.** Every step of the method has a no-AI path. With AI on, the workflow is its own: a conversation on Home that ends in a change-set to confirm, not the no-AI screens reworded.

## 2. The loop

### One order: `dayOrder` (`src/domain/dayOrder.ts`, pure)

- **Takes:** the day's open cards (not waiting, not events, in the area filter), fixed items (`at`, `dur`, `lead`), `planEst`, the running step's `started`, and `now`.
- **Returns:** an ordered list of `{id, kind: fixed|step, start, end}`, `missed[]` (fixed items whose end passed, not done) and `over` (planned minutes that don't fit before the day's end).
- **Orders:** fixed items at `at − lead`; planned steps fill the gaps greedily in planned order. A step that doesn't fit waits for the next gap and a later one that fits moves up. Never split.
- **Takeover:** a fixed item becomes Start here when `now + remaining + margin > at − lead`, with `remaining = planEst − elapsed` (timestamps) and `margin = max(5, round(0.2 × planEst))`. With nothing running, the first step's estimate stands in.
- **Readers:** Start here (Home and Today), Today's list order, the next-step bar, the Calendar's projected blocks, the greeting.

### Morning

1. **The push** at the check-in time: "Morning. Today has the 10:00 dentist and room for about 3 h. Plan it?" (or "A free day so far. Plan it?"). Opens the ritual (no AI) or the conversation (AI on).
2. **No AI: the ritual**, one screen per question, from `checkin.tsx`:
   1. Yesterday in one factual line. Carried steps one at a time: Today / Another day / Drop it / Make it a decision.
   2. Missed fixed items from yesterday, one at a time: Move / Remove.
   3. Shape: from when, until when (presets; `dayEnd` default 19:00).
   4. Pillars: one row per suit, prefilled by the planner; swap or skip. An empty pillar gets one line ("No family step today. Fine, or add one?").
   5. Plan: `dayOrder` with projected times; "3 h 10 of 4 h 30 free"; when over, "About 50 min more than the time you have. Cut one?". Ratio applied. No launch points.
   6. Go: writes the plan and `first`; the first step alone, starter and Start.
3. **AI on: the conversation.** Home opens a planning exchange that runs the today skill's morning check-in in-app (yesterday, shape, a proposal across the pillars with the total against free time, insisting on the first step). It ends in one change-set (`day.update` plus new or broken-down steps) reviewed on the existing review screen. The ritual stays one tap away.
4. **The Calendar** shows planned steps as faded projected blocks from `dayOrder`; they re-flow when a timer overruns.

### During the day

- **Today:** Start here is `dayOrder[0]`; the list follows `dayOrder` with projected times; dragging sets the planned order.
- **Home:** "Coming up" is gone. The greeting, then Start here (or the running card).
- **Missed fixed items:** one small card, "The 14:00 call has passed: Move / Remove". Never red.

### The greeting (no AI, `domain/greeting.ts`)

- Templates in `en.ts`/`fr.ts` with slots from real data; only kinds whose data exists are eligible: the next fixed item ("Anything to move before the 4 pm call?"), a project close to its goal, the time of day, done so far (done only), planned but not started.
- No repeat within a day: a seeded pick; `localStorage` keeps today's shown keys per device (try/catch), reset at the day start.
- Every greeting ends with an invitation to use the box. No counts of missed or carried things. Never database, record, entry or field (unit-tested in both languages).
- With AI on, the greeting stays the template: the AI workflow lives in the conversation, not in the wording.

### Lists

`splitDone(ids)` in `domain/lists.ts` and one `DoneFold` ("3 done ▸"), in Today, each project, each Matrix quadrant and the Calendar day lists.

### Evening

The `close` push; `close.tsx` keeps its show and adds one line per pillar played ("♠ 2 · ♦ 1 · ♥ 1"), the project bars that moved today, and tomorrow's first step. Open steps are not counted.

### Sunday review

- **No AI:** a Goals screen per active project in `review.tsx`: the goal (Still right / Edit / Close), then each open step, "Moves the goal? Yes / No", defaulting to the last answer.
- **AI on:** a review conversation on Home, one project at a time: the AI checks the goal against SMART, says for each open step whether it belongs to the goal and whether it moves it, and proposes changes; you confirm or change them on the review screen.

### SMART goals

Outcome (one sentence), measure (count with target and unit / milestones / none = no bar), start and due dates. Only `advances: true` steps move the bar: count adds 1 per step (editable at done), milestones get ticked (a done advancing step offers "Tick a milestone?"). Card counts never feed it. The old five fields stay stored, folded under More.

### No-AI coverage per method step

| Method step | No AI | With AI |
|---|---|---|
| Urgency/importance | Matrix, ritual pillars | the conversation proposes quadrants |
| Estimates and timing | estimate fields, timer, total vs free time | the same numbers in the conversation |
| Breakdown | Break it down | the conversation breaks down while planning |
| Progress and rewards | done fold, bars, the close | the same |
| Balances décisionnelles | the decision wizard | the decide skill / Ask any AI |
| SMART goals | goal editor, Goals screen | review conversation |
| Problem-solving | I'm stuck, decisions | the conversation names the blocker |
