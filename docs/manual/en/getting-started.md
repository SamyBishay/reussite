# Getting started

## Install it

Réussite is a web app. Nothing to download from a store.

- **iPhone or iPad:** open the address in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open Réussite from there. Notifications only work from the Home Screen.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome. Tap the menu, then Install app.
- **Computer:** open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Chrome and Edge show an install icon in the address bar.

It works offline. Your list lives on your device.

## Setting it up

The first time you open it on a new device, Réussite asks for your language, then how you want an AI to help. Each choice says what it gives you day to day:

![The first screen: language and the three choices](shot:onboarding)

- **AI inside the app (a key).** Plan the day and the Sunday review are a conversation inside the app. It costs a few cents a month, on your own key. Make an account with OpenRouter (one key reaches many models) or Anthropic (a key for its own models), create a key, paste it; the screen shows the steps. The key stays on this device. You tell the AI about your days and it suggests the four things that matter to you, your places and how you want an AI to work with you.
- **Your AI chat app, through the connector.** You plan your day by talking to your AI chat app: it reads and edits your list, with your yes. It needs sync, and an app that accepts connectors of the MCP kind. Set-up first asks for the four things that matter in your days and your places, either in one chat with the app (Réussite gives you a message to copy, and you paste the app's summary back) or by hand. The connector can't change your settings, which is why this part is a copy and paste. Then **Connect your AI chat app** walks you through it: it switches on sync and shows your recovery key to keep, switches on the connector, shows the address to add and the pairing code, with short steps for each kind of app (Claude, or another app that accepts MCP connectors), and checks that the app has come in. You can say **Not now**: it stays under Next steps.
- **No AI.** Plan the day is a few questions, one at a time. Everything works without AI. Four short questions about what matters in your days (what fills your working hours, what keeps your money and papers in order, who and what you look after at home, what you do for yourself), each with a name you can keep or change, then the places you spend time in, such as Home and Work. It takes about a minute.

If you use a chatbot that can't be connected, the link **Any chatbot** under the choices sets you up by copy and paste: Réussite gives you a message to copy into a new chat, and you paste the chat's summary back. Nothing is connected.

Whichever way the AI's suggestion comes back, it is on the review screen, with a tick for each line. Untick what you don't want, or go back and tell the AI what to change. Nothing is written before you tick.

**Skip, use defaults** on the first screen skips the rest and keeps the standard setup. Everything can be changed in Settings, including the look: Réussite starts with the af Klint colours and follows your device for light or dark.

When you finish, Home shows a **Next steps** card with the things set-up doesn't ask: using Réussite on more than one device (and saving the recovery key), reminders on this device, Addresses & people, and connecting your AI chat app if you chose the connector and didn't finish it. Connecting it takes the line away. Each line opens its section in Settings. **Hide this** removes the card, and a line goes by itself once it is done.

If you already use Réussite on another device, choose **I already use Réussite on another device** on the first screen and enter your recovery key. Your places and settings come from the other device, and nothing from this one overwrites them. See [Sync and recovering a lost device](sync-and-recovery.md).
