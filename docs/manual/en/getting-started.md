# Getting started

## Install it

Réussite is a web app. Nothing to download from a store.

- **iPhone or iPad:** open the address in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open Réussite from there. Notifications only work from the Home Screen.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome. Tap the menu, then Install app.
- **Computer:** open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Chrome and Edge show an install icon in the address bar.

It works offline. Your list lives on your device.

## Setting it up

The first time you open it on a new device, Réussite says what it is: the card game patience, where everything you have to do is a card. One button, **Play your first card**, starts. The language is a small switch at the top (it starts in your phone's language).

![The first screen: what Réussite is](shot:onboarding)

1. **The four suits**, already filled in: ♠ **Work** (job or studies, bureaucracy, administrative paperwork), ♦ **Leisure** (fun, relaxation, consumption, hobbies, outings), ♣ **Personal projects** (side projects, chores, research) and ♥ **Interpersonal obligations** (functions and services for family and friends). Every card belongs to a suit, and a card you play lands on its suit's pile. Change a name or a description if another fits you better, or keep them.
2. **What have you been putting off?** One thing, written the way you would say it ("call the bank tomorrow"); tap an example to try one. It becomes your first card.
3. **Its first move.** The first small action that puts you at it, under two minutes and with nothing to work out. Réussite suggests one from the verb ("Open the Phone app" for a call); change it or write your own. Pick the card's suit, then **Deal it**.

You land on Today with that card under **Start here**. Make its first move and start the timer. When you finish, the card lands on its pile, and a small card offers a reminder for tomorrow morning (on an iPhone, it first shows how to add Réussite to the Home Screen, which reminders need). One tap puts that card away for good.

**Not now** on the second screen skips the first card, and **Skip, use defaults** on the first screen skips everything: both go to Home. Everything else is for later and has a sensible default: no cloud AI (Réussite understands you on this device), no sync, no reminders, no calendar link, and your place is "Anywhere" (add your places from the place menu or Settings). The bell on Home lists these as **Next steps**: an AI, sync (and saving the recovery key), reminders on this device, the calendar link and Addresses & people. Each line opens its section in Settings, a line goes by itself once it is done, and **Hide this** removes the list.

**An AI, later.** Out of the box Réussite understands you **on this device**: what you type on Home is read by the app itself, and nothing leaves the device. For **a cloud AI**, open Settings → **How Réussite understands you** (or the **Choose how Réussite understands you** line). It has two ways, and you can use one or both:

- **My own key.** Plan the day and the Sunday review are a conversation inside the app. It costs a few cents a month, on your own key. **Get a key, step by step** shows how: make an account with OpenRouter (one key reaches many models) or Anthropic (a key for its own models), create a key, put it in the box. The key stays on this device. You can then tell the AI about your days and it suggests the four suits, your places and how you want an AI to work with you, on the review screen, with a tick for each line.
- **My AI chat app, through the connector.** **Connect my AI chat app, step by step**: you plan your day by talking to your AI chat app (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another that accepts connectors of the MCP kind); it reads and edits your list, with your yes. The first screen says who can use it in each app. The steps switch on sync and show your recovery key to keep (with one **I've saved it** tap before going on), switch on the connector, say where to add it in each app, show the address and the pairing code, and check that it has come in. Your suits and places stay as they are; change them in Settings.

There is no copy and paste. See [AI](ai.md).

If you already use Réussite on another device, choose **Restore with a recovery key** on the first screen and enter your recovery key. Your places and settings come from the other device, and nothing from this one overwrites them. See [Sync and recovering a lost device](sync-and-recovery.md).

## Your first day

When nothing is planned yet, the bell on Home lists **Plan the day**; it starts the morning check-in. See [The daily loop](daily-loop.md).

## The one idea to keep

Starting is the hard part. Every card in Réussite has a small first move, so the jump from not doing to doing is as small as it can be.

**Research says:** CBT programmes that teach planning help adults with ADHD, and simple if-then plans help people follow through; the two-minute first move itself has not been tested. [See the evidence](research-5.md).

**Practice says:** once work has begun it usually goes fine. What fails is the jump into it, and the jump back in after a break.
