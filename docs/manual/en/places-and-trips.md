# Places and trips

A **place** is somewhere you live, work or stay for a while. Each place gets its own page, and its own clock if it is in another time zone. Steps marked **Anywhere** show on every page.

![The Places sheet](shot:places)

## Switching place

The pill at the top of Today, the Matrix and the other tabs says where you are ("In Home"). Tap it, or open **Places** in Home's menu.

- **I'm here** moves you there.
- **View its page** lets you look at another place's steps and projects, to prepare it, without moving there. A banner reminds you what you're looking at, with a button to go back.
- **+ Add a place** adds one. Give it a name and a time zone.

You can also add places in Settings → Method.

## Areas

An **area** is somewhere inside a place: Heliopolis in Cairo. Add one under the place in Settings → Method → Places, or in the Places sheet (**+ Add an area**). An area has no time zone of its own; it uses its place's. Removing an area puts what was set to it back on the place.

Where you choose a place, the list shows each place followed by its areas. A step in an area is on its place's page too.

When a place has areas, a row of **chips** appears on Today, the matrix and the calendar. Tap one to see only that area's steps, plus those set to Anywhere. Tap it again to clear. The choice lasts until you close the app. Lists are grouped under the area's name.

**Half days.** In the morning check-in, **Where will you be?** lets you pick a place or one of its areas for the morning and for the afternoon. The plan then draws on that area first and keeps its steps together. Today and the calendar show "Afternoon · Heliopolis".

## Stays

A **stay** is a place with a first and a last day. Add one in Settings → Method, under Stays. On the first day Réussite switches to that place by itself, in its time zone. Outside a stay, you're wherever you said you were.

**Before I leave.** A step can be set as "Before I leave", a number of days before the last day of a stay. It turns urgent on its own as the day nears.

## Plan this trip

Under a stay in Settings → Method, tap **Plan this trip**. Réussite builds a pack for an AI that can search the web: your addresses there, what is still to do, what is fixed in time, and your question. Add people you know there only if you tick the box. It is off by default and never includes how to reach them.

![Plan this trip](shot:trip)

Copy the pack into any chatbot. When it answers, come back and tap **Paste the reply**. You see day lists, fixed times and places to try, each with a tick. Places the AI suggests are saved as **Want to go**, with its reason in the note. Nothing is written until you tick it, and everything can be undone from History.

You don't need an AI of your own set up for this. See [AI](ai.md).
