# What's new

## 2.0 — in progress
- Plan the day by hand: a short morning check-in (yesterday, today's shape), then a plan made by the method's rules, which you can swap or cut.
- Break a step down, or tell Réussite you're stuck: it asks which friction, shows the starter, and helps you restart.
- Decisions, start to finish: the problem, every option, weighted pros and cons of doing and of not doing, scores, the choice, and the steps. Réussite reminds you to check it on the date you set.
- The weekly review, one question at a time, ending with how your estimates compared with reality.
- Estimates that learn your pace: after five timed steps, each estimate shows what it usually takes you.
- A Calendar tab, by week or by month. Plan a step on a day, or give it a due date, by dragging or from its menu.
- Places and stays: give each place its own page, and set dates for a trip. Réussite switches place on the first day, and "before I leave" steps turn urgent on their own.
- Repeating steps, steps done on another day, and every timed session can be fixed. Forgot to start or stop the timer? It takes two taps.
- Move steps between the matrix's quadrants, filter, search, and change due dates wherever a step appears.
- Projects get an editor, SMART goals, and a shelf for finished ones.
- Closing the day deals your cards out one by one, readable, with fireworks and a little sound.
- Réussite now runs on its own, without Claude: on iPhone, iPad, Android and computers, and it works offline.
- Your list lives on your device. Settings → Data saves a backup file and restores one.
- Bring your list over from Réussite 1: Settings → Data → Import from Réussite 1. You see what will be imported before anything is written.
- When you mark a step done without the timer, Réussite asks how long it took, with buttons sized to your estimate, a wheel for anything else, and "I didn't time it".
- Links on a step open the website in your browser or the app itself, not a page inside Réussite.
- Choose your theme (light, dark, system or by time of day), colours and text size in Settings.
- French and English.
